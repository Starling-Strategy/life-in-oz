Program Participant: Dacey Brambleley

Theme: Brambleley Machine Shop

Dacey Brambleley is the owner and sole operator of the Brambleley Machine Shop in Saffron Flats. Her practical, no-nonsense approach has made her a go-to fabricator for projects that require equal parts strength, ingenuity, and a keen eye for what will actually work. When a Winkie puppet theatre troupe needed a stage that could survive the rigors of the road, Dacey designed and fabricated a set of modular steel frames. She welded the supports, painted them a durable matte grey, and devised a simple assembly system so the entire stage could be packed onto a single cart. The sturdy platform has since hosted countless performances across the western farmsteads.

Closer to home, Dacey recently tackled a more delicate restoration. A traditional Winkie blacksmith's forge in Saffron Flats, used to train a new apprentice, had a bellows that finally gave out after decades of service. Sourcing the right leather and hand-forming the steel fittings, Dacey fabricated a precise replacement. The new bellows now draws a steady, strong draft, ensuring the apprentice's training in the old methods continues uninterrupted. Dacey's work bridges the practical needs of today with respect for the tools of yesterday.
