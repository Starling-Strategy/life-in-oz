Emerald City Festival Committee
Meeting: Autumn Procession Planning
Date: 15 September 2025
Present: Basil Bramblebourne (Chair), Tessa Finch (Minutes), Kerensa Lanternstead, Lottie Wren, Bennett Cole

1. Opening
   The meeting was called to order by Basil Bramblebourne at 7:05 PM.

Basil Bramblebourne:
Right, thank you all for coming. The autumn procession is our big push now. Summer’s done, the Brickway is clear, and we’ve got a month to pull this together. Tessa’s kindly taking notes.

2. Lantern Update
   Kerensa Lanternstead provided a production report.

Kerensa Lanternstead:
The frames are ready. I have two hundred tin lanterns finished at the workshop on Tinsmith Row. They’re the standard size, with the reinforced hanging loops we agreed on last year. The glass panels will need to be fitted here, of course.

Basil Bramblebourne:
Two hundred! That’s brilliant, Kerensa. That covers the main block of carriers and the lead banners. We’ll need to organize the fitting session. Bennett, we’ll need the glass from storage.

Bennett Cole:
Noted. I’ll have the crates pulled. We’ve got the clear and the colored panes. Just tell me when.

3. Puppet & Carrier Update
   Lottie Wren reported on progress.

Lottie Wren:
The six giant walking puppets are built. The frames are willow from the Gillikin growers, the cloth is dyed, and the faces are painted. We’ve held three training sessions in Yellow Kiln with the twelve carriers. They understand the routes and the basic maneuvers. They’ll need a full walk-through on the Brickway itself, with the lantern carriers, to judge spacing.

Basil Bramblebourne:
Excellent. We’ll schedule that walk-through for the first weekend in October. That gives the carriers time to get familiar with the actual cobbles. Are the puppets stored safely?

Lottie Wren:
They’re in the Kiln community hall for now. We’ll need to transport them to the Emerald City for the final rehearsal.

Bennett Cole:
I can arrange the cartage from the Festival store. We’ll need the large wagon. It came back from the summer fair with a cracked axle, but it’s been repaired.

4. Equipment & Storage Report
   Bennett Cole provided an inventory update.

Bennett Cole:
Speaking of the summer fair, here’s what came back broken and has been dealt with. One folding stage platform—splintered leg. It’s been mended and reinforced. Three dozen flagpole sockets—bent. Straightened and labeled. The main issue was with the rope rigging for the banners: several lengths were frayed. Those have been replaced from stock. All serviceable equipment is cataloged and on the ‘Autumn Procession’ shelves.

Tessa Finch:
Basil, should we note a request for more careful strike procedures after events? To reduce the repair load?

Basil Bramblebourne:
Yes, good point, Tessa. Let’s add that as a standing agenda item for the post-festival debrief. For now, thank you, Bennett, for getting it all sorted.

5. Logistics & Next Steps
   The following actions were agreed:

a. Lantern Glass Fitting (Action: Bennett Cole & Kerensa Lanternstead)
       Bennett to provide glass panes. Kerensa to advise on a volunteer session for assembly. Target date: 22 September.

b. Puppet Transport (Action: Bennett Cole & Lottie Wren)
       Bennett to arrange cartage from Yellow Kiln to Emerald City using the large wagon. Lottie to confirm loading date.

c. Full Procession Walk-Through (Action: Basil Bramblebourne)
       Basil to secure the Brickway permit for the first weekend in October and notify all carriers and puppet teams.

d. Volunteer Coordination (Action: Tessa Finch)
       Tessa to draft the call for lantern carriers and route marshals, to be circulated via the network newsletter.

Basil Bramblebourne:
That covers the major pieces. Anything else? No? Then we’re in good shape. Thank you, everyone. Let’s meet again in two weeks to confirm the walk-through and volunteer numbers.

6. Adjournment
   The meeting adjourned at 8:20 PM.
   Minutes respectfully recorded by Tessa Finch.
