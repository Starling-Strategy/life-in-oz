YOUNG FOUNDERS CIRCLE
Meeting Minutes
June 2026
Present: Calla Thistlestead (Junction Quarter Refill Shop), Xan Cinderby (Cinderby Tutoring), Ulric Poppymore (Blue Orchard Childcare Co-op), Jasper Gatefield (Gatefield Tutoring), Ember Lanternwick (Lanternwick Design Studio), Rowan Bell (Community Manager).

1. Welcome and updates from the network
   Rowan Bell provided a brief update on upcoming Brickway Transit Union forums and reminded members of the Summer Festival Committee's call for volunteers.

2. Member-led project updates
   2.1 Xan Cinderby reported on the new peer-review system implemented at Cinderby Tutoring. Students now exchange draft essays or problem sets with each other for constructive feedback before their final one-on-one sessions. Xan noted that this has fostered collaboration and improved self-editing skills, with several students reporting they feel more confident reviewing their own work.

   2.2 Jasper Gatefield reported on his successful bulk purchase. He negotiated with a surplus dealer in Winkie Country to acquire thirty refurbished graphing calculators. These are now available for students to borrow for a semester at a fee covering only the cost of replacement batteries. Jasper stated this removes a significant financial barrier for students in advanced mathematics courses.

   2.3 Ember Lanternwick presented the completed artwork and layout for the Quadling Harvest Festival program booklet. She managed to fit the full schedule, a detailed map of the festival grounds, and the complete vendor list into a tidy eight-page foldout design. The print-ready files have been delivered to the Festival Committee.

3. New business and proposals
   3.1 Calla Thistlestead proposed a new collaborative initiative. She offered the use of the side room at the Junction Quarter Refill Shop to host a monthly pop-up for the Timber Verge Repair Co-op. The idea is that community members could bring in small appliances—kettles, lamps, radios—for assessment and repair by co-op members on a designated Saturday each month. Calla emphasized the shop's central location and foot traffic as potential benefits.
   - Discussion: Jasper Gatefield asked about liability for repairs. Calla responded that the pop-up would operate under the Timber Verge Repair Co-op's existing membership and insurance structure. Ember Lanternwick suggested clear signage to manage customer expectations about wait times and repairability. Ulric Poppymore noted the value of keeping useful items out of the waste stream.
   - Decision: The circle endorsed the proposal in principle. Rowan Bell agreed to connect Calla with the leadership of the Timber Verge Repair Co-op to discuss logistics and scheduling.

   3.2 Rowan Bell relayed a piece of hearsay from the broader network regarding Ulric Poppymore's work. Several Meadow Mutual Aid members mentioned that Ulric helped resolve a long-running debate within the Blue Orchard Childcare Co-op concerning nut-free snack policies. Ulric drafted a clear, posted guideline that satisfied all parties.
   - Ulric Poppymore confirmed the account, adding that the process involved listening to parent concerns about both allergy safety and cultural food practices, and that the final guideline focused on designated 'allergy-aware' tables and clear labeling for shared snacks.

4. Open discussion and resource sharing
   - Xan Cinderby inquired if other founders had experience with simple contract templates for freelance or part-time help. Ember Lanternwick offered to share a basic service agreement her studio uses.
   - Jasper Gatefield mentioned the surplus dealer might have other educational materials available in bulk and would circulate the contact if others were interested.
   - Calla Thistlestead reminded the group about the upcoming Refill Shop anniversary event in late July.

5. Next steps and actions
   - ACTION: Rowan Bell to connect Calla Thistlestead with the Timber Verge Repair Co-op coordinator. (Due: July 15)
   - ACTION: Ember Lanternwick to share contract template with Xan Cinderby. (Due: June 30)
   - ACTION: Jasper Gatefield to circulate surplus dealer contact details to the circle mailing list. (Due: June 25)

6. Next meeting
   The next meeting is scheduled for Wednesday, July 22, 2026, at 6:00 PM, in the Reading Room Network's Blue Room. The agenda will include a review of the proposed pop-up collaboration progress.
