Notes from my conversation with Wilfred Spindlebourne, 21 May.

Wilfred said he used leftover rebar from a foundry job to make a metal trellis for his neighbor’s climbing beans. It was just a few pieces cut and bent to a good shape, welded at the joints. He said the beans grew right up it all season, no trouble with the wind. The neighbor was pleased.

I mentioned my own thought, about maybe doing fluoride varnish treatments at a busy Winkie Country food pantry. Not a full clinic, just a quick application on distribution days. Wilfred listened and said it sounded like a solid plan. He asked if I’d need any special permission from the pantry folks. I said I haven’t asked them yet. It’s still just an idea I’m turning over.
