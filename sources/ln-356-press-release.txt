Wizard's Office Liaisons Highlight Community Impact Across Oz

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
October 2025
Emerald City, Oz

The Wizard’s Office Liaisons, a circle within the Emerald City Leadership Network, today highlighted recent member-driven initiatives that have delivered concrete results for residents in Gillikin, Quadling, and Winkie communities.

In the Emerald City, Records Clerk Zora Oakholt resolved a long-standing property issue by uncovering a mislabelled box in the Gillikin Country archives. “It was a standard metal box, tucked behind newer files,” Oakholt said. “The labels had faded, the location was wrong, and the deed bundle inside was out of sequence.” Her discovery provided the necessary documentation for a land query that had been delayed for over a year, clearing the way for a boundary confirmation between two farming families.

Zora Oakholt:
It was a matter of checking the old index, then the shelf list, then the storage log. Three lists, and the third one had the clue.

In Quadling Country, Community Health Worker Bea Reedcombe organized a free hearing test session at the Hammerhead Rise community hall. An audiologist from the Emerald City conducted tests for forty-seven older residents, identifying several who would benefit from further consultation. The event also provided information on managing everyday sounds and maintaining ear health.

Bea Reedcombe:
People were hesitant at first. But once one person sat down and had a quiet, straightforward chat with the specialist, the line formed. It’s about access, and dignity.

In Winkie Country, Electrician Ansel Greenbourne addressed a recurring problem at a small pottery studio in Yellow Kiln. The studio’s kiln had been producing inconsistent results due to frayed and overheating control wiring. Greenbourne replaced the old wiring with higher-temperature rated cable, restoring reliable firing cycles for the artists.

Ansel Greenbourne:
The original cable wasn’t rated for the ambient heat inside the kiln cabinet. It was degrading, causing voltage drop and erratic element behavior. The new cable solves the thermal problem. The kiln heats evenly now.

Rowan Bell, Community Manager for the Emerald City Leadership Network, noted the common thread. “These actions aren’t grand gestures. They are the essential, skilled work that keeps daily life moving—clearing a bureaucratic logjam, connecting people to health resources, fixing a critical tool. The Liaisons circle exemplifies how local expertise, shared through the network, creates tangible reliability.”

The Wizard’s Office Liaisons circle connects civic employees and community-based professionals across Oz to share knowledge and collaborate on projects that improve local systems and services.
