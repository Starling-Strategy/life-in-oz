The first few loads were a huge hit—the good, dense stuff that lasts. Really fills a gap.

Nothing official had been posted yet, but a few commuters got a heads-up and were able to arrange car shares ahead of time. Saved a lot of evening frustration at the depot.

Lena Thistlecombe:
