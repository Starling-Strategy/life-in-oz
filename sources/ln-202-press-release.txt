Kettle Canteen Owner Responds to Fridge Failure with Vigilance, Turns Farm Surplus into Community Asset

Theme: Kettle Canteen

FOR RELEASE
MEADOWBANK, Munchkin Country – March 2025 – When the walk-in refrigerator at the Kettle Canteen failed overnight last month, owner Beatrice Kettle faced a difficult but clear choice.

Beatrice Kettle:
Everything that had climbed above the safe line went straight into the compost. It hurt to see it go, but you can't serve what you wouldn't eat yourself. The repair crew got us cold again by noon, but that wasn't the end of it for me.

Kettle instituted a twice-daily temperature log for the unit, a practice she has continued even after the compressor was fully repaired. "I'm checking it morning and night, without fail," she says. "It's added to the closing routine. If it starts to drift again, we'll know before we lose a single carrot."

In a separate initiative this month, Kettle transformed a potential waste problem into a community resource. A neighboring farm donated a large surplus of tomatoes that were too soft for market but perfectly ripe.

Beatrice Kettle:
They were beautiful, just a bit past their shelf life. I couldn't let them go to the pigs—not that the pigs wouldn't love them—but I thought, we can do better. I stayed late, roasted them all down, pureed them, and got them into the freezer. Portioned out, labeled. The community kitchen down the road can pull a container anytime they need a base for a soup or a stew. No fuss.

The resulting sauce, frozen in meal-sized containers, has been made available to the Meadowbank Community Kitchen for use in their prepared meal program. Rowan Bell, Community Manager for the Emerald City Leadership Network, of which Kettle is a member, noted the dual approach.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Beatrice sees both sides of the work. There's the necessary discipline to protect what you have, and then there's the generous instinct to stretch a windfall to serve more people. It's practical stewardship.

The Kettle Canteen remains a popular gathering spot in central Meadowbank, known for its generous portions and daily specials.
