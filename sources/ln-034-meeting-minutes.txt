Wizard's Office Liaisons Circle Quarterly Meeting
Date: 15 April 2024
Present: Dilys Clovermore, Marcus Wren, Bea Reedcombe, Nadia Stone, Otis Orchardholt, Elspeth Wickham, Rowan Bell (Community Manager, ECLN)

1. Welcome & Introduction
   Rowan Bell welcomed members, noting the focus of the meeting was to share recent work and coordinate with the Wizard's Office on community-facing processes.

2. Updates from the Wizard's Office
   2.1. Dilys Clovermore (Community Grants Officer) reported that the new one-page template for small grant reporting is now in circulation. It asks for a simple listing of expenditures against the grant’s purpose and a signature, removing the requirement for recipients to submit a full accounts pack. The template is being distributed through the South Meadow and Junction Quarter advice desks.
   2.2. Elspeth Wickham (Records Clerk) reported on the genealogy research request backlog. She implemented a basic ledger system to log incoming requests, assign them a number, and track their stage. Since its introduction in February, the average time to provide a response has been reduced from six weeks to three. The system uses a simple paper ledger and colored tabs.

3. Member Updates & Shared Practices
   3.1. Marcus Wren (Tenant Support Clerk) shared an experience from late winter. A tenant with limited Munchkin was struggling with the winter heating allowance form. Marcus created a visual guide by cutting icons from an old catalog—a picture of a house for 'address', a flame for 'heating cost', a person for 'applicant'—and pasting them next to the relevant form fields. He walked through it with the tenant. The application was submitted and was accepted by the Wizard's Office without any further queries.
   3.2. Nadia Stone (Survey Assistant) reported on equipment maintenance. After a transit bump during a survey in South Copse, she noticed a slight wobble in one of the tripod legs. She disassembed the leg joint, found the locking collar had loosened, tightened it with the provided key, and then verified the alignment by checking the spirit level against a known flat surface. The tripod is now back in service.
   3.3. Otis Orchardholt (Beekeeper) described assisting a novice beekeeper from Redfern Hill who was concerned about a gray, powdery mold on some internal hive wood. Otis examined a sample, noting its scent was earthy, not sour, and the texture was dry. He correctly identified it as a common, harmless fungus that appears in damp springs, preventing the beekeeper from applying an unnecessary and potentially harmful hive treatment.
   3.4. Bea Reedcombe (Community Health Worker) outlined a plan for the coming month. She intends to set up a small information stall at the next Hammerhead Rise market. The stall will distribute the latest Quadling Health Access newsletter and she will be available to answer questions about the upcoming seasonal flu shot program, including locations and times.

4. Discussion: Improving Access to Forms and Information
   - Dilys noted that Marcus's visual guide approach was an excellent example of simplifying access. She asked if Marcus would be willing to share the guide template, even if just as a photo, so other advice desks could adapt the idea.
   - Marcus agreed, noting he would need to create a new version without the copyrighted catalog images, perhaps using simple hand-drawn symbols.
   - Elspeth suggested that the genealogy request ledger system, while simple, could be adapted for other small-office tracking needs where a digital system is overkill.
   - Nadia raised a point about the durability of field equipment, suggesting a quick visual check protocol after any transit.

5. Action Items
   A1. Marcus Wren to create a shareable version of the visual form guide and send it to Dilys Clovermore by 30 April.
   A2. Bea Reedcombe to confirm the date of the Hammerhead Rise market with the Festival Committee and inform the Liaisons list once the stall is confirmed.
   A3. Rowan Bell to circulate the one-page grant report template and a description of the genealogy ledger system to the broader ECLN network via the next digest.

6. Next Meeting
   The next quarterly meeting is scheduled for July 2024. Proposed agenda item: reviewing the impact of the simplified grant reporting template.

Meeting closed at 4:15 p.m.
