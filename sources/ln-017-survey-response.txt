Network Members Survey – Festival Committee Focus

Thank you for taking a moment to share your thoughts. Your input helps shape our collaborative efforts.

1. What’s a project you’re currently most excited about within the Festival Committee's scope?

Milo Plumton:
Well, I’m really excited about the autumn concert series we’re planning. The music’s coming together beautifully, but I keep thinking about the logistics. I intend to propose to the committee that the band invest in a custom-fitted rain canopy for the main bandstand. Last year we had that sudden shower just as we started the second set, and it was a scramble. A proper canopy would mean we could play through a light rain without worrying about the instruments. It’s a practical thing, but it makes all the difference for a smooth performance.

2. Are there any resources or connections you feel the network could help facilitate for your work?

Alma Reedton:
Connections, absolutely! I’m planning a small spring concert to be held in the North Orchard public gardens. The acoustics there are just lovely when the trees are in blossom. It’s pending approval from the Festival Committee and the gardens’ manager, of course. If the network has any existing relationship with the gardens’ management, or knows someone on the committee who’s particularly keen on outdoor music, that would be a wonderful introduction. Otherwise, we’ll knock on the door politely and see what blooms!

3. How do you see your skills or interests contributing to the wider network community?

Oriel Brambleworth:
I’ve been thinking about skill-sharing a great deal lately. My work is in puppetry and props, which is a very specific kind of making. I’ve offered to mentor a Winkie Works Guild apprentice who’s expressed an interest in theatrical prop construction. It’s pending a workshop schedule—finding a time when my shop is clear and they can travel. Teaching someone how to reinforce a joint or choose a lightweight wood… it’s like laying a foundation. If that foundation is solid, they can build anything they imagine, and that strengthens the whole community’s capacity for beautiful, durable work.

4. Is there anything else you’d like the Festival Committee or the network’s community manager to know?

Milo Plumton:
Just that I’ll have some sketches and quotes for that canopy ready for the next committee meeting. It’s an investment, but one that will serve us for many seasons to come.

Alma Reedton:
If the North Orchard concert comes off, I’d love to invite all network members. It would be a perfect, relaxed way to celebrate the season together.

Oriel Brambleworth:
Only that I’m grateful for these forums. Hearing about the band’s plans and Alma’s concert reminds me that festival work is a tapestry—different threads, different colors, but all part of the same picture.
