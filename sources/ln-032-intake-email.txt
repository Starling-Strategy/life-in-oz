Ezra Winkleley:
Zeb, you asked for examples of procedural tweaks. This is a minor one, but it had a measurable effect. Last month, I redesigned our firm's standard invoice templates. The old ones had the payment terms buried in the footer in a small font. The new version has a clear, bold box at the top right with the due date, net amount, and late-payment details. We've been using them for three weeks now, and the number of client calls asking 'When is this due?' has dropped by about half. It's just clearer. Thought it might be of interest. —Ezra
