Rhoda Reedshaw:

And from my end, over at the Quadling Community Theatre, they had a bit of a panic. A pipe backstage let go just enough to spray the heavy velvet stage curtains—the big maroon ones they use for the winter plays. They were sure the water stains were set for good.

They brought them to me in a heap, damp and worried. It was a two-day job: a careful cold-water wash, a slow dry in the big room, and then a lot of patient steaming and brushing to bring the pile back up. No harsh chemicals, just time and elbow grease. Got them back to the theatre last Friday, good as new. The stage manager was so relieved she almost hugged me, but settled for a firm handshake and a promise of free tickets.

It's nice when the work is about saving something, not just cleaning it.

Rhoda
