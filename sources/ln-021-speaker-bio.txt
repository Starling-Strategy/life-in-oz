Meet the Speakers: Festival Committee

Format: speaker_bio
Theme: Festival Committee

Bennett Cole serves as the Festival Committee’s equipment custodian, managing the storehouse in Bell Market. His dry, inventory-minded approach and fondness for clear labels ensure that every marquee pole, cable, and folding table is accounted for and ready. Recently, when a large festival marquee showed a torn corner panel, Bennett reinforced it with a heavy-duty canvas patch and waterproof seam sealant, extending its usable life for at least another season of weekend events.

Felix Rowe is a producer for Bell Market community radio, bringing a fast-talking energy and a keen ear for a good clip to the airwaves. His resourcefulness extends beyond the mixing board; when the studio’s oldest headphones began to fail, Felix cut and glued replacement foam pads himself, restoring their seal so producers could monitor audio without bleed.

Jellia Jamb is a translator and community-radio host for Violet Hills community radio in Gillikin Country. Warm on air but exacting about wording, she often switches languages mid-sentence, reflecting the neighborhood’s lively mix. Jellia plans to launch a weekly five-minute radio segment featuring simple phrases and greetings in the different languages spoken across the county, aiming to foster basic, friendly communication among neighbors.

Oscar Diggs works as an exhibition technician at the Junction Quarter Reading Room, where he maintains the listening corner with a showman’s honest patter and a technician’s practical readiness—he always brings a backup. His quick action recently saved critical equipment: Oscar patched a persistent leak in the roof directly above the electronics with a sheet of salvaged copper flashing, preventing damage to the playback gear during the last rainstorm.
