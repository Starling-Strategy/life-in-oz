From: Lydia Hart
Notes for Gillikin Growers, August

Item 1: I've been looking at the cider apple bottleneck we always get in October. I intend to propose a dedicated afternoon shift just for sorting cider apples. Run it from two till six, separate from the main grading line. Would take the pressure off the morning crew and let us process the windfalls and smaller fruit without holding up the fresh market grading. Need to check if we can get enough hands, but I think the students from the local school could manage it.

Item 2: Heard from the Meadow Mutual Aid coordinator that Hollis Cinderton from Reed Flats had a surplus from his herd last week. He didn't let it sit. He loaded up several crates of fresh milk and drove them over to the community pantry at Brickway Junction himself. The pantry folks were apparently very glad to see it.
