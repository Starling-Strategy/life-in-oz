Willa,

Thank you for asking. The punch bowl for the opening ceremony was a minor crisis averted. It’s a large, old family piece the organizers borrowed, and the spout had snapped clean off in transit. A clean break, but in an awkward, weight-bearing spot.

I used a series of internal ceramic dowels for alignment and structural support, then sealed it with a food-grade adhesive. The repair is nearly invisible from the outside, and critically, it held the weight of the punch for the entire ceremony without a drop leaking. The owner was relieved, and the committee didn’t have to scramble for a last-minute replacement.

Sincerely,
Vera
