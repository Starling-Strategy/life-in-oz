Oscar Diggs:
Subject: That winter listening corner

Hi Rowan,

Just wanted to close the loop on that little project from last winter, the one for the reading room. You might remember the request to make a small listening corner for local memories. It’s been a while, but I’m still pleased with how it landed.

I set it up before either of the folding-panel jobs came along, so it stands apart from the orchard work and the budgeted traveling display. It was a modest setup: a bench, a pair of quality headphones on a stand, and a small placard. The room asked for something that let visitors hear, not just read. I recorded two consenting adult residents recalling their first visits to the Emerald City. I handled the recording, edited a short excerpt, and then played it back to both contributors. That step mattered to me. I wanted to make sure they were comfortable with the final cut before anyone else heard it.

One of them asked to remove a specific detail that identified a shopkeeper’s name. I made the edit, re-listened to ensure the flow wasn’t broken, and got their okay again. We kept it simple. I set the approved excerpt to loop quietly for the open afternoon.

I was there for part of the afternoon. I didn’t count heads or track usage stats, but I did watch a few people sit down. One visitor closed her eyes for a bit, just listening. Another leaned over to a friend and said something about the sound of the gates. It felt right. A small space, a clear voice, no demand for more.

I have an idea for a similar setup for the community garden, maybe using seasonal sounds instead of memories. It’s just a plan for now, but I wanted to let you know. No rush. Thanks for the help getting the room’s requirements sorted.

Best,
Oscar
