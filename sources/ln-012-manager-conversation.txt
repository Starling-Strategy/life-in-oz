Rowan Bell — community manager:
Isla said she's been busy with the early apple runs from Gillikin. She coordinated with three orchards—Blue Hill, Riverbend, and the old hillside orchard—to get their harvest collected and into her refrigerated trailer in under two hours. She had to adjust her route twice to hit the five-hour window for the juicing plant in Munchkinland. She says they got the whole load in, and the plant supervisor sent a note that the brix levels were perfect for pressing.

Isla Poppywood:
You plan for the road, but you can't plan for a yard. I was at the Winkie Works mill last week, and the driver ahead of me had a pallet tip right as they were pulling out. Grain sacks everywhere. We got the forklift driver to lift the pallet back, and I ran and got my spare cargo straps. We salvaged about forty sacks that were still sealed, restacked them, and I strapped his whole load down for him so he could finish his delivery to the bakery co-op. He was only an hour behind schedule.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Isla mentioned the other driver was from a non-network outfit, just grateful for the help. She said it was standard procedure, nothing special.
