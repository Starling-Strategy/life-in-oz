Dear Committee,

I hope this finds everyone well after the recent events. I’m writing separately from the usual festival planning to share a proposal I’ve drafted for Quadling Health Access. It suggests offering clinic appointments one evening per week, specifically for patients whose work or care responsibilities make daytime visits difficult. The proposal outlines staffing, security, and lighting considerations for the South Meadow Clinic. It is currently under review by the Quadling Health Access steering group.

On a different note, I heard from a few of you at the last meeting about Quill Tincombe’s recent work. It sounds like the city’s recycling campaign has a charming new figurehead, courtesy of his workshop.

Best regards,
Uri Pepperbourne
Clinic Manager, South Meadow Clinic
