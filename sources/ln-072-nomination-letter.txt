Nomination for Iolo Winkleton - Winkie Works Guild Member of the Year

Theme: Winkie Works Guild

Yves Brambleholt:
To the Founders’ Gala Selection Committee,

I’m writing to nominate Iolo Winkleton for recognition at this year’s event. I’m Yves Brambleholt, of Brambleholt Upholstery, and I’ve only been a member of the Winkie Works Guild for a couple of years. But in that time, the person I’ve seen who most embodies the guild’s purpose—making the work easier and better for everyone—is Iolo.

My own recent contribution is a small one: I provided a quote to reupholster the worn pew cushions at a small Quadling church, suggesting a foam core wrapped in brushed cotton for durability. It’s a job I’m planning to do, and it’s the sort of quiet maintenance work that keeps a community space usable. But Iolo’s work made me think about what ‘usable’ really means on the floor of a busy shop.

Iolo reorganized the entire maintenance tool crib at the Saffron Flats Can Works. He hung all the wrenches on a perforated board, labeled clearly. I heard from a mechanic who works there that it cut the average time they spend searching for a tool by more than half. That’s minutes saved every day, for every person on a shift. That’s less frustration, fewer jobs started late because someone was hunting for the right size. It’s a simple, physical solution that shows deep respect for his coworkers’ time and effort.

Iolo doesn’t make a fuss. He just sees something that isn’t working as well as it could and fixes it. That’s the spirit I think the guild exists to celebrate. He’s made the work at the can works measurably better, and that deserves recognition.

Sincerely,
Yves Brambleholt
Brambleholt Upholstery
