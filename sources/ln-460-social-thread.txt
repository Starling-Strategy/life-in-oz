Kit Amberworth:
Morning all. Quick story from the 42 line yesterday. Found a beautiful little wool project left on the back seat—looked like a sock halfway done. But it was the needle case that caught my eye, this bright blue enamel with little yellow flowers. I remembered a passenger working on it earlier, one of our regulars who gets off at the Green Market stop. Held onto it at the depot, and sure enough, she came by this morning looking a bit frantic. The smile on her face when I handed it over was worth the extra trip to the locker. She said her grandmother gave her that case. Just a good reminder to check the seats, folks.

Jem Haymere:
Tool sharpening night was good. Got a grinder from the smithy down in Bluebell Crossing to come out. He set up in Bay 3. Took about two hours. Everyone's personal wrenches, drill bits, a few scribers. Makes a difference. Cleaner cuts, less strain. Should do it quarterly.

Adele Barleyton:
Re: the Old Gillikin Bridge closure next week. Timetable adjustments are posted on the depot board and are live in the schedule books. Routes 12, 15, and 18 will divert via the Quadling Bypass and the Munchkin Connector. Transfer points remain at Brickway Junction and Meadowbank Station. Adds seven to nine minutes depending on time of day. Please remind passengers of the change. It's only for seven days.

Bertram Violetridge:
Jem's sharpening night got me thinking about efficiency in a different way. I'm proposing we start a simple log. A clipboard in each cab, just for the steeper hills on the Winkie and Gillikin runs. Driver notes the hill, the gear they used, and an estimate of fuel used from the gauge drop. No fancy tech. After a month, we pool the logs. Aim is to see if there's a consensus on the most efficient shift pattern for, say, Violet Hill versus Timber Verge. Could save a tank a month across the fleet. I'll draft a sheet.

Driver:
Bertram, I like that idea. Violet Hill in third gear always feels like it's lugging, but fourth screams. Be good to have some data instead of just my gut.

Kit Amberworth:
I'd be happy to test that log on my 42 route. We've got that long pull up from the Quadling flats. And Adele, thanks for the heads-up on the bridge. I'll start warning my regulars today.
