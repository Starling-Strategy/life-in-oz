Rowan Bell — community manager:
Festival Committee post-event debrief - 15 April 2026
Attending: Rosa Clay, Pim Plumstead, Aurelia Ashmere, Toby Brickwick, Ansel Wickby. Rowan Bell (noting).

1. Welcome arch banner – Pim reported the banner is ready. She used a plant-based dye (she wouldn't say which, trade secret) to bring the velvet back to a proper emerald. It took three separate dips to get the color even, but it's now hanging in her studio, dry and deep green. She'll deliver it to the site the morning of the ceremony.
2. Toby's Reading Room event – Toby said the lunchtime talk went well. About twenty people came. The naturalist brought pressed leaves from the Timber Verge and the Meadow to show different canopy layers, linking them to which birds stop where. One older passenger from the Brickway line asked several detailed questions about warbler sightings. Toby noted we could use a better map for next time; the one on the wall is from before the last Brickway extension.
3. Aurelia's salvage project – Aurelia reported the books are dry. She separated the pages of two full boxes, using newsprint from the Herald's old stockroom. Most are saved. A few with heavy cardboard covers are still warped, but the paper pages are intact. She's thinking some of the saved illustrations could be scanned for the Festival's children's activity sheets.
4. Ansel's contribution – Ansel confirmed he's available for on-site repairs during the Festival weekend. He mentioned a recent job: replaced the drive belt on an old washing machine for a customer in Timber Verge. Had the part in his stock. Said the principle is the same for any moving part on our temporary stages – listen for the squeal, find the wear. He'll bring his kit.
5. Studio sustainability – Rosa floated an idea for the Committee to consider later. She plans to propose using crushed bisque scraps (failed pots that are fired once) as drainage material in the studio's potted plants, instead of buying gravel. It's porous and we have buckets of the stuff. She'll bring a sample to the next meeting.

Open actions:
- Pim to deliver banner 27 April, 8 AM.
- Aurelia to liaise with the school about using scanned images, confirm permissions.
- Ansel to review the stage mechanism plans sent by the construction lead.
- Rosa to prepare bisque-scrap sample and a short write-up for the proposal.
- Toby to source a current map of the city and surrounding counties for future events.
