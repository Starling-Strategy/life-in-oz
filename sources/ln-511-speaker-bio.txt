Maren Hedgemore, Cooperative Buyer for Gillikin Growers Cooperative, lives in Violet Hills. Her work ensures growers get fair prices and members receive quality goods. Recently, after a shipment arrived with numerous broken handles, Maren sourced a skilled wicker-worker in Quadling Country to craft sixty replacement harvest baskets, negotiating a favorable rate that saved the cooperative funds and kept the harvest season on schedule.

Kai Tinmere is the community fridge keeper for the Reed Flats community fridge in Munchkin Country. Kai focuses on making sure the fridge is stocked and accessible to all neighbors. Fellow volunteers mention that Kai recently began filling out paperwork for a grant to improve the fridge's insulation, though the task was interrupted by the need to sort and store a large, unexpected donation of root vegetables from a local farm.

Rafe Ashbourne coordinates the Gillikin Falls Childcare Co-op, bringing a warm and diplomatic approach to managing the needs of children, parents, and caregivers. Looking ahead, Rafe has proposed a plan to convert an underused corner of the co-op's outdoor yard into a small, shared garden plot. The intention is for the toddlers to help plant peas and sunflowers there when the weather turns next spring.
