Nomination for Lottie Wren, Festival Committee Contributor

Format: nomination_letter
Theme: Festival Committee

Willa Rushley:
To the Founders' Gala Committee,

I'm writing to nominate Lottie Wren for recognition at this year's gala. I've worked alongside Lottie on Festival Committee logistics for the past two seasons, and her quiet, practical approach to solving problems is exactly the kind of contribution that makes our network stronger.

My own recent work feels like a good example of the scale Lottie operates on, which is why I mention it. For the Redfern Hill Autumn Festival setup, I spent an afternoon with a marker and a poster board at the community center, working out a carpool schedule for volunteers coming from the outlying farms. It was simple, but it got over a dozen people to the site and back home each day without a single missed ride. That kind of basic coordination—making sure people can get where they need to be—is the foundation everything else gets built on.

Lottie understands that foundation instinctively. Last month, she noticed that volunteer carriers for her puppet workshop in Yellow Kiln were getting lost on the final turn. Instead of just sending more detailed instructions, she painted and installed a proper wooden directional sign pointing the way. It's durable, it's clear, and it's already stopped the weekend rehearsal confusion cold. She saw a friction point for people trying to help and smoothed it out permanently.

That's Lottie. She doesn't make a fuss; she makes a fix. Her work ensures that the volunteers, who are the lifeblood of our festivals and circles, can focus on the task instead of the trouble of finding it. I can't think of a better quality to celebrate. Please consider Lottie Wren for this honor.

Sincerely,
Willa Rushley
