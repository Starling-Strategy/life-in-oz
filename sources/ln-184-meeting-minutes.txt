Reading Room Network Committee Meeting
Date: 25 February 2025
Location: Junction Quarter Reading Room
Present: Rowan Bell (Community Manager), Greta Vane (Junction Quarter Reading Room), Jellia Jamb (Violet Hills Community Radio), Felix Rowe (Bell Market Community Radio), June Arden (Munchkinland Herald)

1. Call to Order and Agenda Review
   Rowan Bell called the meeting to order at 6:05 PM. The agenda was approved without amendment.

2. Updates on Member-Led Projects
   Members provided brief updates on recent activities related to the network's resources.

Greta Vane:
I've instituted a simple tracking system for our periodicals. A small wooden date stamp is now placed beside the magazine subscriptions. When a new issue arrives, I stamp the cover with its renewal month. It's visual, it's clear, and it meant we caught three titles that were about to lapse last year.

Greta noted the system requires no extra paperwork and has been well received by regular patrons who now understand the renewal cycle.

Jellia Jamb:
For the Violet Hills seed swap, I did daily announcements on air for a week. Translated them into the local dialect, of course—times, location, how to clean and label your seeds. 'Bring them in a paper packet, write the name clearly.' We had the highest turnout in years. Many, many clearly labeled packets.

Jellia emphasized that the clarity of the instructions, broadcast in the community's primary language, was key to the event's smooth operation.

Felix Rowe:
Right, my turn. I borrowed that reel-to-reel player from the network's shared equipment list. Spent a whole afternoon with a box of old festival interview tapes—voices from, what, fifteen years ago? Transferred them to digital files before the oxide flaked off any further. Got them all. Good quality, too.

Felix confirmed the tapes are now preserved and the digital copies will be added to the Reading Room's local history archive.

June Arden:
The Munchkinland Herald published a correction. The initial notice for the Old Mill renovation grants had a typo in the application window—listed the wrong month. I obtained the correct dates from the grants office and we published the correction, with the right application window and the right decision date.

June stated the correction was run prominently to ensure no applicants were misled by the earlier error.

3. Discussion: Accessibility of Archival Materials
   The committee discussed how to make preserved materials, like the digitized tapes, more accessible to patrons with varying needs.

Jellia Jamb:
If we have transcripts, I can help with translation. Or summaries in different languages. Not everyone reads the Gazette dialect easily.

Felix Rowe:
The files are clean. I can produce short clips—good for radio spots, too. 'This week in the archives...' That kind of thing. A good clip draws people in.

Greta Vane:
We would need a clear labeling system for any physical copies or listening stations. A patron shouldn't have to ask three questions to find what they need. A stamped date, a language code—something straightforward.

4. Action Items
   a. Felix Rowe will provide the digital audio files to Rowan Bell for network distribution by 10 March.
   b. Jellia Jamb and June Arden will collaborate on drafting a multi-language summary template for archival audio materials, to be reviewed at the next meeting.
   c. Greta Vane will draft a simple physical labeling protocol for archived materials, incorporating her stamping system logic, for committee email review.

5. Next Meeting
   The next meeting is scheduled for 25 March 2025, location to be confirmed.
   Meeting adjourned at 7:20 PM.
