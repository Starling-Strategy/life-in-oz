Rowan Bell — community manager:
Hannah called about the storm response below Brickway Junction. The county road washed out on the Monday and stayed closed for nine days.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Asked what happened, the neighbors mostly talked about Dorothy Gale. Nobody remembers who put her in charge. Hannah passed on what one of them told her: “We were all standing around the depot, and by Tuesday she had forty people on a list, each with a job, before anyone had asked her to.”

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Gale Agricultural Supply's two trucks ran the back lanes twice a day. Food, feed and medicine reached every farm on the cut-off side. I have this from Hannah; I haven't spoken to Dorothy yet.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Hannah would like Dorothy recognized at the spring meeting. Nothing is arranged. Follow up in April.
