Emerald City Leadership Network
Member Experience Survey – July 2025

This survey helps us understand how our network supports your work and community.

1. In the last few months, have you worked on a project or solved a problem related to your place of work or neighborhood? Please describe what you did.

Priya Dell:
Yes, two things. First, at the store: last Tuesday morning I got in early and saw a big limb from the old sweetgum tree had come down right across the back alley where the supply truck pulls up. It was delivery day, and the driver can’t maneuver around that. I got the hand-saw from the storeroom—the one we use for cutting down boxes—and cleared enough of it to get a clear path. Took about forty minutes, and I had the brush stacked by the compost bins before the truck even turned onto Birch Lane. Kept the day on schedule.

2. Have you contributed to maintaining or improving a shared community resource or public space? Please describe.

Priya Dell:
That’s the second thing. I volunteer for the bird surveys along the Kalidah Gorge. Two weeks back, on my usual route, I found a section where the spring rains had completely washed out the path. It was unstable. I had some bright orange tape in my pack, so I marked a safe detour through the ferns about ten yards uphill. Took pictures with my camera to show the scale of the washout and sent them to the route stewards that evening. They said the photos were clear enough for the repair crew to prioritize materials.

3. How, if at all, has being part of the Emerald City Leadership Network connected you to resources or people that helped with these kinds of tasks?

Priya Dell:
The Timber Verge Repair Co-op circle shared a guide on safe branch removal after a storm last year. I remembered it—check for tension, start with smaller limbs. I didn’t need to call anyone, but knowing the guide was there meant I wasn’t just guessing.

Thank you for your responses.
