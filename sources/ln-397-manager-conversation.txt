Rowan Bell — community manager:
Fergus said the mending evening last week went well. He cleared the folding tables to the side and set up the good light over the pressing station. He provided thread from his own stock and kept three irons hot on the commercial presser so folks could finish their seams. Said about a dozen people came, mostly from the Reed Flats road crews and a couple of gardeners from Meadowbrook. They worked on patching knees and reinforcing pockets. Fergus estimates it saved at least three families from having to buy new work trousers right now, which is a real help with winter coming.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Separately, he mentioned tracking down a misdelivered quilt for an older customer in Meadowbrook. The parcel had gone to the wrong van, then ended up at the central postal depot in the city. Fergus said he spent an afternoon making calls, describing the quilt’s distinctive log-cabin pattern to three different clerks. He finally traced its path, drove to the depot to collect it himself, and delivered it to the customer's doorstep. The customer was very relieved; it was a family heirloom. Fergus said the postal service thanked him for the detailed tracing notes.
