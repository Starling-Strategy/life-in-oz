For the community planning meeting last week: Prepared eighty mini vegetable pies using surplus root vegetables (carrots, turnips, a few purple potatoes) from a Gillikin Growers member who had more than they could store. The pies were a hit—the clerk from the planning board said they disappeared before the first agenda item was finished. No leftovers!

Separately, for the Timber Verge winter market volunteer setup this past Saturday: Made two large insulated dispensers of spiced apple tea for the crew. It was a particularly chilly morning, and the road crew member managing the barricades said it kept their hands warm during the two-hour setup. They drained both dispensers by 9 AM.
