Intake Notes: A Traveler from Kansas Arrives in Munchkinland

SYNTHETIC SOURCE — original fictional text; no real event or publication.
Format: manager_conversation
Theme: Welcoming services and privacy

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Spoke with Boq at the Munchkinland township hall. He reported that a young girl named Dorothy Gale arrived unexpectedly this morning after a severe cyclone carried her family's farmhouse into the district.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Boq said Dorothy was traveling with her small dog Toto and was eager to get home to her family in Kansas. When local residents gathered to see what happened, she told them plainly: "I am not a sorceress. My house blew down in the cyclone, and I could not help where it landed."

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Boq and the northern regional liaison advised her to follow the yellow brick road to the central district to consult the administration. Dorothy thanked the council, put on a pair of silver walking shoes, and set out immediately.
