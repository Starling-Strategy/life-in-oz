Rowan Bell — community manager:
Called Barnaby Stonefield on April 17. He’s deep in planning for the summer.

Barnaby Stonefield:
The thermoboxes are the big project. We’ve got twelve of them that are starting to look rough—dents, rust spots, the gaskets are shot. I’ve been talking to the Winkie Works Guild about pooling labor. My thought is we get a crew together over the summer, sand them down, repaint them, replace all the seals. It’s a lot of hours, but if we can do it, it’ll keep them running for another few seasons at least.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
He emphasized this is still a proposal, waiting for Guild buy-in on scheduling.

Barnaby Stonefield:
Separately, I did get a smaller thing off the ground. Last week, I pulled together a few of our regular drivers and some from the Guild for a meetup at that truck stop cafe on the western Brickway. Seven of us total. The rain’s been a menace, so we swapped tips on securing tarps and tying down loads when everything’s slick. Good, practical stuff. We also agreed to start a simple cardboard check-in system at the Reed Flats depot—just a clipboard with a sheet to mark when you’ve dropped off or picked up a shared load. Cuts down on the morning confusion.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
The drivers present agreed to the check-in system. Barnaby said it was useful to hear the Winkie drivers’ methods; they have different knots for different materials. He sounded tired but pleased with how it went.
