Rowan Bell — community manager:
Maren reported on two recent activities at the Gate Quarter Reading Room. First, she spent last Thursday afternoon clipping job ads from recent editions of the Emerald City Gazette and the Munchkinland Herald. She organized them by trade—delivery, kitchen work, shopkeeping—and pasted them into a labeled binder. She said it’s already on the resource table, and a few learners have used it to practice reading real postings.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Separately, she mentioned a learner who’s been coming in for a few months, wanting to read recipes. Maren found a set of simple illustrated cookbooks in the collection—the ones with big pictures of vegetables and short instructions. She pulled those out and now meets with that learner weekly to go through a recipe together. She said the learner brought in a spice cake last week from one they’d read.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Maren noted the binder needs restocking as the clippings get handled, and she plans to keep an eye out for more ads next week. She also said she might ask around if anyone has spare cooking magazines with pictures.
