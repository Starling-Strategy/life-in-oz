Meet the Speaker: Basil Coppershaw

Format: speaker_bio
Theme: Coppershaw Solar

Basil Coppershaw founded Coppershaw Solar to bring reliable, locally maintained power systems to homes and businesses across Oz. He's known for his ability to translate complex electrical concepts into something a neighbor with a toolbox can understand. Last month, when the Spectacle Lane secondary school's technology teacher mentioned her students were struggling to visualize full-system layouts, Basil stayed up late drafting. He delivered a simplified, large-format wiring diagram that mapped a typical home solar installation from panels to battery, using color-coding and clear icons instead of dense schematics. The teacher reports the poster is now permanently mounted in the shop, and students use it to trace circuits with their fingers.

Looking ahead, Basil plans to put that same practical mindset toward a community project. He has offered to conduct a free, preliminary solar assessment for the roof of the Clay Hollow community centre. The goal is to gather concrete data—square footage, orientation, shading estimates—to strengthen their upcoming application for a green infrastructure grant. 'If the numbers are there,' he says, 'we can build the case.'
