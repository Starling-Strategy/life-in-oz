Wade Emeryfield coordinates adult education for the Junction Quarter Learning Rooms. His warm, encouraging manner and deliberate pace help students build confidence alongside new skills. Last autumn, his four-week 'Everyday Budgeting' class was so oversubscribed that he arranged for a second tutor and split the group into two concurrent sessions, ensuring everyone who signed up could attend.

Hollis Wheatfield is the librarian at the Lantern Row Reading Room, where a quiet, precise attention to detail preserves the collection for future readers. Recently, she carefully repaired the cracked leather binding and resewed the signature of a rare 19th-century botany folio from the Reading Room's special collection, a task requiring patience and a steady hand.

Rufus Brambledale is a freelance translator whose precise work bridges practical gaps between Oz's communities. In a recent project, he translated a feed-supply contract for a Gillikin growers' cooperative, clarifying ambiguous clauses about delivery timelines that had been a source of dispute in previous seasons, providing much-needed clarity for both growers and suppliers.
