Hi Gus,

Wanted to let the Guild know about a small thing from my route. Monday morning at the Blue Orchard shelter on the southern Brickway spur, I noticed one of the tall glass panels was completely shattered. Big pieces on the bench, little ones everywhere. Not safe for anyone waiting, especially with kids.

I pulled over at the next stop and used the union portal on my tablet to file a repair. Gave them the exact stop number, uploaded two photos, and noted the morning passenger volume. Got the auto-reply, didn't think much more of it. But when I came through on Tuesday, the crew had already been out. They'd cleared all the glass and put up those heavy canvas barriers around the broken frame until the new panel comes in. It's not pretty, but it's secure. The dispatcher mentioned the detailed request helped them prioritize it. Just glad it's handled.

Hope you're well,
Pella

For the record,

The bakery equipment importer in Green Market finally received their shipment of those large commercial ovens from the western Winkie country. The maintenance manual that came with them was, to put it kindly, written in a very technical local dialect. Lots of regional terms for components and procedures that don't translate directly.

They hired me to produce a clear, actionable version in Emerald City standard. Finished it last week. The trick was keeping the precise technical meaning for the repair technicians while making the safety warnings and step-by-step guides completely unambiguous for the bakery owners. Delivered the final document; the client confirmed everything was clear and they've already circulated it to the first three shops that took delivery.

A satisfying bit of precision work.

Gus
