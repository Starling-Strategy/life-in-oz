Subject: On cardstock and ledgers
Date: 23 May 2025

First, regarding the school. A teacher from Bell Market stopped in yesterday for some pencils. She mentioned, while paying, that the box of heavy cardstock I’d given them last month worked out perfectly for a model-building project. It was left over from a print job for the Chamber—good stock, too heavy to just recycle. She said the students used it for building little bridges and towers. That’s all I know about that.

Separately, the Timber Verge Repair Co-op. Their clerk came by looking for account books. They’d been using loose sheets, which is no way to run a business. I gave them a set of three hardbound ledgers on credit. Told them to keep their numbers in proper books, and to pay me when they could. They seemed grateful, if a little intimidated by the columns. That was a few weeks back.
