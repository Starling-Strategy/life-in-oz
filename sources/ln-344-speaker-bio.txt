Event Program: Meet the Organizers

SYNTHETIC SOURCE — original fictional text; no real event or publication.
Format: speaker_bio
Theme: Meadow Mutual Aid

Calla Copperby (she/her) coordinates the delivery rota for Meadow Mutual Aid, ensuring members in Bluebell Crossing and surrounding Munchkinland hamlets receive food staples, medicine, and library books. Her approach is famously practical: when the group's cargo bike developed a crack in its main frame, she didn't wait for a grant. She took it to a local welding shop and funded the repair through a direct collection from her neighbors, keeping the bike—and the deliveries—rolling.

Phoebe Greenworth (she/her) manages the Saffron Flats community pantry in Winkie Country, a hub that serves over fifty households. Her cheerful energy is a pantry staple. This past winter, after a miscommunication led to a confusing first draft of the holiday hours, she cheerfully reprinted the notice twice, making sure the final version was posted clearly at every corner store and community board, proving that getting it right is more important than getting it done fast.

Delphine Pepperford (she/her) is the coordinator of the Bluebell Crossing Childcare Co-op, where her talent for organization and lists keeps everything from schedules to snack rotations running smoothly. During a particularly relentless week of rain, when outdoor play was impossible, she helped the children construct an elaborate indoor obstacle course from cushions, blankets, and play tunnels—an activity that became such a favorite it's now a regular part of the co-op's rainy-day plans.

Omby Amby (he/him) serves as volunteer coordinator for the Emerald City food pantry, a role that suits his earnest, methodical nature. (You can reliably find the day's plan posted on the pantry door by seven each morning.) When a minor basement seepage threatened the lower shelves, he quickly organized a crew to move all the salvaged dry goods into temporary crates. Thanks to his clear instructions and steady presence, pantry service was back up and running within a single day.
