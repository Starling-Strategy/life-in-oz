Speaker Bios: Fernwick Machine Shop

Theme: Fernwick Machine Shop

Albin Fernwick owns and operates Fernwick Machine Shop in Timber Verge. A fixture of the Winkie Works Guild, he is the person members call when a part breaks down and there’s no time to wait for a factory shipment. Last month, when a greenhouse builder was stalled waiting for custom mounting brackets, Albin personally drove the finished order to the site, offering a discount on the next batch to make up for the delay. In a separate job, a grain mill’s conveyor system seized when a steel roller failed days before the peak harvest. Albin machined a replacement from scratch, working late to have it delivered and installed before the week’s first truckload arrived. His shop motto, seldom spoken aloud, is simply to get the work done.
