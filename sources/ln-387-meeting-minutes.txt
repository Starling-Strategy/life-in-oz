Emerald City Festival Committee
Meeting Minutes
Date: November 4, 2025
Present: Cosmo Hazelshaw, Greer Saffroncombe, Milo Plumton, Rowan Bell (Community Manager)
1. Review of Agenda
   The meeting was called to order at 7:00 PM. Rowan Bell reviewed the agenda, focusing on logistical updates for the upcoming Emerald Lights Festival.
2. Transportation and Cleanup Logistics
   Greer Saffroncombe reported that Brickway Transit has successfully implemented an extra late-night bus loop for festival cleanup crews. The route circles the market squares twice, beginning one hour after the main festivities conclude. She noted that drivers have been briefed and signage will be posted at the central depot and the three main market square stops. 'The first test run last week went smoothly,' she said, 'though we did adjust the timing by ten minutes after seeing how long it took a crew to pack their brooms and bins.'
3. Parade Float Lighting
   Cosmo Hazelshaw volunteered to design and install the lighting for two community parade floats. He stated his focus will be on battery-powered LED systems to increase safety and mobility. 'No trailing cords to trip over or to require a generator cart,' he said. 'It means we can be more flexible with the float assembly points.' He plans to source materials this month and begin assembly workshops in early December, inviting other committee members to assist. The design phase is pending final float dimensions from the carpentry co-op.
4. Cultural Exchange and Music
   Milo Plumton provided an update on musical preparations. He reported that he successfully organized a trade of traditional march sheet music with the Quadling Reed & Bell community band. 'We sent them three of our favorite Gillikin country marches, and they've shared two beautiful, rhythmic pieces from the southern marshes,' he said. 'Our band has already started rehearsing one, and the harmony parts are fantastic.' He mentioned this exchange will directly enrich the repertoire for the festival's opening procession.
5. Open Discussion and Action Items
   - Rowan Bell will follow up with the carpentry co-op to secure final float dimensions for Cosmo by November 18.
   - Greer will distribute the finalized late-night bus schedule to all registered cleanup crew leads by November 15.
   - Milo will coordinate with the Quadling band liaison to confirm they have received the sheet music shipment.
   - All members agreed to review volunteer sign-up lists for their respective areas before the next meeting.
6. Next Meeting
   The next Festival Committee meeting is scheduled for December 2, 2025, at 7:00 PM.
Meeting adjourned at 8:20 PM.
