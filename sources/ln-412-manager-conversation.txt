Rowan Bell — community manager:
Reached out to Magnus for an update on the Wizard's Office's public notices work and to discuss a proposal he'd mentioned.

Magnus Greendale:
The parking map situation, yes. A clerk in the print room told me they'd run a batch with the wrong dates for the Brickway market closures. I heard about it after they'd already been collated. Rather than reprint the whole run, I spent an afternoon with a date stamp and an ink pad at the back table. Corrected about fifty of them before they went out to the branch libraries. Tidy enough work, if a bit tedious.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Noted the hands-on correction. Asked about the proposal for festival forms.

Magnus Greendale:
That's the bigger picture item. Right now, if a neighborhood circle wants to block a lane for a harvest supper or a block party, they might need to file a Notice of Temporary Occupancy, a Public Assembly Waiver, a Non-Commercial Vending Permit if there's food, and a separate Noise Variance if there's music. All different forms from different divisions. I've proposed—it's just a draft on my desk—a single 'Small Community Gathering Application.' One form, one fee schedule, routed internally. It would cut down on confusion for applicants and clerical work here. Still just a proposal.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Acknowledged the potential efficiency gain. No timeline yet for review. Call ended with thanks.
