Alma Plumfield is a carpenter and the proprietor of Plumfield Joinery in Saffron Flats. Her work is defined by a thoughtful precision that turns persistent problems into durable, elegant solutions. This past year, she volunteered her skills to support the Winkie Works Guild's fundraising efforts, constructing a custom donation box. The design—a sturdy, lockable cabinet with a clear acrylic panel for visibility—was both secure and inviting, a practical upgrade the guild credits with a noticeable increase in contributions.

Alma's community-minded approach extends beyond formal projects. When a neighbor mentioned a garden shed door that had sagged and scraped for years, Alma stopped by with her tools. In a single afternoon, she realigned the frame, used shims to correct the angle, and installed new hinges. The door now swings smoothly, a small but lasting fix that erased a daily frustration. Alma brings this same blend of clear-eyed diagnosis and quiet craftsmanship to every discussion about repair, maintenance, and building things meant to last.
