Oren Cinderley is the steward of the Violet Hills tool library in Gillikin Country, a role that suits his patient and precise nature. He recently demonstrated his dedication to shared resources by tracking down a long-overdue pruning saw, which had been missing for months. Following a hunch and a few quiet inquiries, he found it rusting under a berry bush in a neglected corner of the Blue Orchard community plot. Oren cleaned, sharpened, and oiled the saw before returning it to the library's active inventory, a small but essential act of care for the community's common tools.

Fennel Wheatstead owns the South Copse Bicycle Shop in Quadling Country. His detail-oriented and slightly formal approach serves him well in both repair work and community collaboration. This past season, recognizing that several shops were facing the same rising costs, he negotiated a joint bulk order of commuter tires with two other Quadling bike shops. His patient work on the logistics and vendor communication achieved a better wholesale price for all three businesses, a practical example of mutual aid in the small business community.

Faye Wickcombe is a no-nonsense electrician who runs Wickcombe Electrical in Timber Verge, Winkie Country. Known for her concise assessments and dry wit, she brings a practical eye to the co-op's operations. Looking ahead, Faye is proposing that the Timber Verge Repair Co-op organize a bulk buy of copper wire. Her plan is to pool the purchasing power of several local tradespeople to lock in a better price for materials needed in the next quarter, aiming to keep project costs manageable for everyone.

Dinah Violetworth, an electrician based in Yellow Kiln, Winkie Country, brings a direct and reassuring presence to her work. Her community focus extends beyond her client list. With the busy harvest season winding down, Dinah intends to visit four elderly neighbors on her street to test their kitchen and bathroom outlets for proper grounding, free of charge. This planned effort reflects her commitment to ensuring safety and peace of mind for those who might not think to ask for a check-up.
