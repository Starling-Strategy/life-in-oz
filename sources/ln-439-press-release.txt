BLUEBELL CROSSING, Munchkin Country – The Brickway Transit Union’s Bluebell Crossing depot has resolved two persistent maintenance issues, restoring full functionality to key service areas and ensuring reliable accessibility features for passengers.

For months, the weekly cleaning shift at the depot’s bus wash bay was hampered by a slow drain that caused significant delays. Doris Plumbourne, a depot mechanic, identified and cleared a deep clog composed of accumulated leaves and congealed grease. The bay can now be cleared efficiently after each cleaning cycle.

Doris Plumbourne:
It was just a matter of getting the right tool down there and being stubborn. Now the water goes where it’s supposed to, and the crew can get on with their day.

In a separate, scheduled effort, the depot team also completed the quarterly inspection and lubrication of all kneeling bus ramps housed at the Bluebell Crossing facility. Doris Plumbourne assisted in the work, which ensures the ramps deploy smoothly and reliably for passengers who need them.

Doris Plumbourne:
Those ramps get a lot of use. A little preventive care keeps them working right and saves a bigger repair down the line. It’s straightforward work, but it matters.
