Now their van picks up my flower buckets for the Lattices, the Corner Cup, and the Bramble Cafe on Tuesdays and Fridays. Saves me a trip, gets the pastries and the flowers there before the breakfast rush—it just makes sense.

So I just pulled some peonies and ranunculus from my display coolers, added some silvery greens, and tied it up. Had it ready before the ceremony started. It wasn’t the planned bouquet, but she said it was perfect.
