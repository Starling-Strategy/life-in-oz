Ines Emeryby:
Hello everyone,

A quick note about my own small contribution. I realized our paper log for tracking instrument sterilization cycles at the practice was getting quite muddled—dates were hard to find, and it was easy to miss a batch. So, I made a new wall chart with clear columns for the date, cycle number, and which instrument sets were processed. It’s laminated so we can use a grease pencil. It’s just a simple chart, but the assistants say it’s much easier to check at a glance now.

---

Milo Winkleham:
Milo here. While we’re sharing minor fixes, I had a go at the old work lights for the Lantern Row community theatre. The metal housings on six of them had cracked right along the seams, probably from being knocked over one too many times. I cleaned the cracks thoroughly, filled them with a high-temp epoxy, and then ran a strip of aluminum tape over each seam for a bit of extra reinforcement. They’re not pretty, but they hold the bulbs securely again. Should get them through another few seasons of setup and strike.
