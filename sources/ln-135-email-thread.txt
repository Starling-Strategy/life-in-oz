Pavel, Eleanor,

I’ll be frank—this is the sort of thing that makes me nervous. Coordinating multiple businesses on one route sounds simple until someone’s order isn’t ready on time or the van breaks down. The risk of someone getting let down is real.

But. The math is plain. And I’ve just seen a version of this work on a bigger scale. I didn’t mention it much, but Bracken Freight won the contract for the South Copse school transport routes this fall. It’s a set schedule, multiple stops, different schools. We had to be reliable. We are.

If you move forward, I can share what we learned about route planning and buffer time. You’d need clear agreements, in writing, about windows and responsibilities. The hard thing is sticking to the plan, but saying the hard thing upfront is how you avoid the mess later.

I’m not in Blue Orchard, so I won’t join, but I’m happy to talk logistics.

Leona Bracken
