Timber Verge Repair Co-op Steering Committee Minutes
Date: 17 February 2026
Present: Kezia Quadbourne, Pia Oakridge, Alma Plumfield, Rowan Bell (Community Manager)

1. Call to Order & Review of Previous Minutes
   The meeting was called to order at 7:05 PM. Minutes from the January meeting were approved.

2. Member Activity Reports
   Kezia Quadbourne reported on work completed for the Clay Hollow community hall. She diagnosed a faulty thermostat in the hall's water boiler last Tuesday. She sourced a compatible replacement part and completed the installation on Wednesday afternoon. The boiler is now functioning correctly, providing reliable hot water for the hall's weekly tea gatherings. Kezia noted the hall's treasurer has processed the co-op's invoice.

   Alma Plumfield reported on a project for the Saffron Flats community hall. She was contracted to inspect and repair the wooden stage ahead of their annual harvest dance. She identified three rotten planks along the front edge and several weakened joints. She replaced the planks with seasoned timber from her own stock and reinforced the joints with new brackets and fasteners. The work was completed over two days last week. The hall committee has confirmed the stage is now structurally sound for the event.

3. Tool Library Update
   Pia Oakridge provided the monthly report on tool library usage. Circulation remains steady, but late returns continue to be an issue, particularly for power tools.
   Pia proposed a new initiative to improve timely returns. She plans to create a simple reminder system using pre-printed postcards. The plan is for the tool library steward to fill out a card with the tool's description and due date when it is checked out. If the tool is not returned after one week past its due date, the steward will mail the postcard as a reminder. Pia believes this physical prompt will be more effective than the current verbal reminders. She requested a small budget to cover the printing and postage for an initial batch of 100 cards. Alma seconded the proposal. The committee approved the budget allocation, pending a review after three months.

4. Co-op Capacity & Scheduling
   Discussion turned to managing incoming requests. All three members currently have full schedules for the next four weeks. The committee agreed to update the co-op's answering service message to indicate a longer wait time for non-emergency assessments. Kezia suggested creating a simple priority checklist (e.g., safety hazard, community facility, elderly resident) to help triage calls when Rowan Bell is doing intake.

5. Financial Report
   Rowan Bell presented the monthly financial summary. Income from completed jobs in January was strong. The tool library's maintenance fund is adequate. The new postcard budget was noted as a forthcoming expense.

6. New Business & Open Actions
   - ACTION: Pia Oakridge will draft the postcard design and obtain printing quotes by March 3.
   - ACTION: Rowan Bell will update the phone message and create a draft triage checklist for review at the next meeting.
   - NEXT MEETING: Scheduled for 17 March 2026, 7:00 PM.

7. Adjournment
   The meeting was adjourned at 8:20 PM.
