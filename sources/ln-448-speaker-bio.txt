Vera Porcelain

SYNTHETIC SOURCE — original fictional text; no real event or publication.
Format: speaker_bio
Theme: Porcelain Mends

Vera Porcelain runs Porcelain Mends, a studio specializing in the repair of ceramic, porcelain, and pottery in Clay Hollow, Quadling Country. Her work is a quiet art of restoration, bringing valued pieces back to use without a trace. Recently, when the shared kiln that serves many local artisans in Clay Hollow malfunctioned, Vera’s schedule was disrupted by a week. Rather than delay her clients, she organized a batch of pieces needing firing and transported them to a repair shop in the next valley, ensuring her commitments were met. In a separate project, she was entrusted with a large, borrowed punch bowl for the Clay Hollow harvest festival’s opening ceremony; its ceramic spout had shattered. Vera meticulously reconstructed it using a series of internal ceramic dowels and a food-grade sealant, a repair so sound the bowl was used without hesitation for the festival’s toast.
