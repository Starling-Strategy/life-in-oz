The Yellow Brick Board · storm relief · March 2025

Road crew member:
Whoever resoled our boots this week, thank you. Some of us were walking on split soles by Thursday.

Kerensa Hedgecombe:
Six of my crew, to be exact. That was Ruth Fenwick at the cobbler's on Lantern Row. She did forty pairs in a week and wouldn't take money from the crews.

Neighbor:
She did my husband's too. He's been on the Brickway clearing since Monday and she had them back to him the next morning.

Ruth Fenwick:
Couldn't have you out there with wet feet. Bring them back if the stitching goes.
