For Lux: She was doing a big corporate lunch last week for that new office tower team. The client sent over the allergy list, but the printer at the office must have been acting up—it cut off the bottom line that listed the nut allergies. Lux caught it because the number of 'no's' didn't match the headcount she was given. She didn't just flag it; she had her team pivot on the spot and prep a whole separate set of nut-free platters from her backup ingredients. Client never knew there was almost a problem. Hannah at the Chamber office told me about it; she knows the office manager.

For me: Finally got the Bell Market on Brickway to let me take their used crates. They were just getting tossed into the recycler, all cracked and dirty. Now I collect from that one and two others on Tuesdays and Fridays. I wash them, let them dry proper on the racks (no mildew!), and I've started returning the good ones to a couple growers out near the Orchard instead of them having to buy fresh cardboard boxes every season. It's only about forty crates a week right now, but the growers seem pleased. It's a start!
