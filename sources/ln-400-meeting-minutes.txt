Gillikin Growers Circle Monthly Meeting
Date: December 12, 2025
Location: Heatherford Community Hall
Present: Kai Kilnby, Henry Dale, Posy Oakdale, Ivy Hedgeton, Edith Reedstead, Pia Reedford, Rowan Bell (manager).

1. Welcome and Opening Remarks (Rowan Bell)
Rowan Bell opened the meeting at 7:05 PM, welcoming members and guests. The primary focus of the meeting was to share updates on recent projects and discuss proposals for the coming season.

2. Reports on Recent Activities
   2.1 Tool Shed Reorganization (Ivy Hedgeton)
   Ivy Hedgeton reported that she has reorganized the cluttered tool shed at the co-op. She labeled all shelves clearly and installed hooks for hanging long-handled tools like shovels and rakes. Members noted that this has already saved volunteers time in locating equipment for the soil testing workshop last week.

   2.2 Youth Agriculture Tour (Edith Reedstead)
   Edith Reedstead reported that she hosted a tour of the Violet Hills Grain Mill for a Gillikin Growers youth agriculture class. She demonstrated the entire milling process, from the grain hopper to the bagging station, for the group of fifteen students. The students asked many questions about grain varieties and stone grinding.

3. New Proposals and Offers
   3.1 Backyard Hive Workshop (Kai Kilnby)
   Kai Kilnby proposed... a short, hands-on workshop about starting a backyard hive. The workshop would focus on sourcing essential equipment and identifying local nectar sources... in the Heatherford area. He suggested a Saturday in late March, pending interest.

   3.2 Compost Thermometer Loan System (Henry Dale)
   Henry Dale is proposing... a compost thermometer loan system for local gardeners. He is drafting a simple sign-out sheet to be kept in the tool shed. The goal is to allow members to check their own pile temperatures to ensure proper decomposition. He will source two thermometers to start.

   3.3 Flower Subscription Service (Posy Oakdale)
   Posy Oakdale is thinking about... starting a weekly flower subscription for a few households in Heatherford. She described a simple system using reused glass jars for delivery, which she said would be like 'a perennial promise, showing up on your step each week.' She will survey interest among neighbors first.

   3.4 Tool Repair Offer (Pia Reedford)
   Pia Reedford has offered... to lend her mechanical skills to the Timber Verge Repair Co-op on her next weekend off. She specifically mentioned helping to fix community garden tools such as wheelbarrows and pruning shears that require welding or part replacement.

4. Discussion and Decisions
   - The group agreed to support Kai's workshop proposal. Rowan Bell will help secure a location and promote it through the network newsletter.
   - Henry's thermometer loan system was approved. Henry will purchase the thermometers and have the sign-out sheet ready by the January meeting.
   - Posy's flower subscription was met with enthusiasm as a potential model for other growers. No formal support was requested, but members offered to share surplus jars.
   - Pia's offer to assist the Timber Verge Repair Co-op was noted as a valuable cross-circle collaboration. Rowan will connect her with the co-op's lead.
   - The group thanked Ivy again for the tool shed work, noting the improvement in workshop setup efficiency.

5. Open Floor / Other Business
   - A member asked about sourcing bulk mulch for spring. Edith Reedstead mentioned the Violet Hills mill often has spent grain hulls available.
   - The date for the next meeting was set for January 16, 2026.

6. Adjournment
   The meeting was adjourned at 8:30 PM.

Minutes submitted by Rowan Bell, Community Manager, Emerald City Leadership Network.
