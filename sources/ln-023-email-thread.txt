Anselm, that’s brilliant news about the lighting. Congratulations.

To follow up on what I showed the group: the chalkboard panels are done. I built five framed panels, each about the size of a window shutter. The key was sealing the frames—I used a clear weather-resistant varnish on the wood and set the chalkboards back slightly under a lip. Should stand up to a light drizzle so vendors can write the day’s prices without the board running. They’re simple, but they have a clean look. I have them at my workshop in Clay Hollow whenever the market coordinators want to pick them up.

Julian
