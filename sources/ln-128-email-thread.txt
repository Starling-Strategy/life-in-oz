Hi Rowan,

Thanks for reaching out. We did manage to get our winter hall situation settled. We were looking at the rental costs for the community hall where we run our afternoon programs, and it was going to be a real pinch with our budget. So we sat down with the hall committee and proposed a trade: we'd take over the basic cleaning after our sessions and handle our own setup and breakdown if they could reduce our rate. They agreed. It’s a fair swap—we save on fees, and they save on custodial hours. It means our parents and volunteers pitch in a little extra at the end of the day, but we’re all happy to do it to keep the co-op running smoothly through the season.

Hope that’s the kind of thing you were looking for.

Best,

Rowan,

Regarding your request: it was a small thing, but it was causing a persistent issue. At the Brickway Junction depot, we have an older-style fare box on one of the backup trams. For months, we'd been finding the coins inside were corroding, which is a problem for counting and for the machines later on. I tracked it down to a very slow leak—condensation was getting in overnight. The original gasket had perished and the part wasn't made anymore.

I took the measurements and sourced a custom-cut rubber gasket from a supplier over in the Quadling market district. It was a simple fix once we had the right piece. It seals properly now, so the revenue is protected and we don't have to spend time cleaning corroded coinage. It should last for years.

Regards,
Leif Barleycombe
