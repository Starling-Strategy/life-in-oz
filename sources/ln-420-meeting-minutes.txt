Timber Verge Repair Co-op Steering Committee
Meeting held via telephone conference
Date: January 12, 2026
Present: Mabel Cinderwood, Ember Thistlewood, Doris Emerycombe, Dov Quadford, Alma Plumfield, Rowan Bell (Community Manager, ECLN)

1. Call to Order
Mabel Cinderwood called the meeting to order at 7:05 PM.

2. Approval of Previous Minutes
The minutes from the December 8 meeting were approved by consensus.

3. Member Updates & Skills Share
Members reported on recent work and community contributions.

   a) Dov Quadford reported that he completed the rewiring of the stage lights at the South Copse community hall last month. He replaced several frayed cables and installed the new dimmer switch that was donated from the co-op's shared inventory. The hall's events committee has confirmed the system is now fully operational.

   b) Doris Emerycombe reported that she had to turn down a contract last week to fabricate thirty sections of ornamental fence for a new housing development in Blue Orchard. The design specifications required sections over four meters long, and her current workshop lacks the clear floor space needed for assembly and the secure outdoor storage for pieces of that length prior to installation.

   c) A story was shared from the co-op's general membership chat: Ember Thistlewood was reportedly seen at the Bell Market last fall using the handle of a metal spoon as a temporary brake cable adjuster on a child's bicycle. The child had a loose cable and couldn't brake properly. Ember's quick fix allowed the child to ride home safely, where a proper replacement part could be fitted.

   d) Word has also come through the co-op network regarding Alma Plumfield. It was mentioned that she repaired a full set of wobbly desks for a primary school in Saffron Flats over a weekend in late December. She is said to have asked only to be reimbursed for the cost of the new fasteners and wood glue used.

   e) Mabel Cinderwood announced an offer to the co-op and the wider Emerald City Leadership Network. She is offering free preliminary consultations for any Emerald City small business considering rooftop solar panel installation. The consultation would focus on initial load assessment and grid connection feasibility, to help owners understand the scope before they seek formal bids.

4. Co-op Capacity & Resource Discussion
The committee discussed the space limitations raised by Doris Emerycombe.
   - Ember Thistlewood suggested the co-op look into potential short-term rental of a larger workspace in the Munchkin Country area for specific large-scale projects, splitting the cost between the co-op and the client.
   - Dov Quadford noted that storage is a chronic issue for fabrication work and asked if any members had contacts with secure yard space.
   - Action: Rowan Bell agreed to query the wider ECLN network via the next newsletter for leads on affordable, secure temporary workshop or storage space in the Munchkin Country region.

5. Solar Consultation Offer Coordination
   - The committee endorsed Mabel's offer and agreed to help promote it.
   - Action: Ember Thistlewood will draft a short blurb for the co-op's section of the ECLN February newsletter, describing Mabel's consultation offer and how interested businesses can contact her.
   - Action: Mabel Cinderwood will provide Ember with the key points to include regarding what the consultation covers and any prerequisites for businesses.

6. Tool & Material Bank
   - Alma Plumfield reported the adhesive and fastener sections of the shared inventory are depleted and need restocking.
   - Action: Doris Emerycombe and Dov Quadford will review the inventory list and compile a purchase order for common consumables by January 26. Alma will handle the procurement using the co-op's petty cash fund.

7. Next Meeting
The next meeting is scheduled for February 9, 2026, at 7:00 PM, via telephone conference.

8. Adjournment
Mabel Cinderwood adjourned the meeting at 8:20 PM.

Minutes submitted by Rowan Bell, Community Manager, ECLN.
