Omby says the Saturday shift was humming along until one of the hand-dollies started shimmying badly on the Brickway. A volunteer was about to abandon a crate of carrots. He flagged her down, pulled a wrench from his tool bag, and tightened the axle nut right there. He says the sound of that wheel rolling smoothly again was better than the lunch bell.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Separately, he’s been working on the bread supply. He talked to the baker at Gate Quarter Gables. They agreed the pantry will send a volunteer every Tuesday to collect day-old loaves in a big waxed canvas tote the bakery provided. Omby says it’s not a huge quantity, but it’s dependable, and it means every parcel gets a starch now.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
He posted the new Tuesday collection duty on the volunteer board this morning. He’s pleased it didn’t require any new funds, just a handshake and a tote.
