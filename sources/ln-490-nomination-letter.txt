A Nomination for Glinda Southwell

SYNTHETIC SOURCE — original fictional text; no real event or publication.
Format: nomination_letter
Theme: Founders' Gala honorees

Amir Brook:
To the Founders’ Gala Selection Committee,

I’m writing to nominate Glinda Southwell for an honor at this year’s Gala. I know the network celebrates people who build connections and solve problems in practical ways, and I can’t think of anyone who embodies that more quietly and effectively than Glinda.

I speak from direct, somewhat awkward experience. Last spring, the owner of the bakery next to my repair shop and I were at a complete impasse over a shared exterior rolling shutter. It’s an old building, and the shutter mechanism covers both our storefronts. We couldn’t agree on a schedule for opening and closing it—I needed it up later for clients picking up repairs, she wanted it down earlier for security. We’d stopped speaking, and it was starting to affect our businesses and the mood of the whole block.

Glinda didn’t come in with a solution. She sat with each of us separately in our own shops, just asking questions about how our days worked and what we were worried about. Then she brought us together in her calm way and helped us hear what the other was actually saying. It wasn’t about the shutter; it was about predictability and respecting each other’s time. We worked out a seasonal schedule that changes with the daylight, and we wrote it down. A year later, that schedule still holds. More importantly, my neighbor and I can talk again.

What Glinda did for a pair of stubborn shopkeepers in Junction Quarter, she does every day for families, co-ops, and circles across the South Meadow and beyond. She fixes the human machinery so the rest of us can get on with our work. She’s a foundational part of what makes this network strong, and I hope you’ll agree she deserves this recognition.

Sincerely,
Amir Brook
Brook Device Repair
