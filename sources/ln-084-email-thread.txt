Hello everyone,

Milo, that’s a lovely offer for Violet Hills.

On our end, the annual flu shot clinic in Hammerhead Rise is done. We tried something new this year. I compiled a printed, large-print roster with fifteen-minute time slots for the whole access day. We posted copies at the community board and the library a week ahead. People could come by my desk, look at the sheet, and put their name in a slot that worked for them.

It made a real difference. On the day, there was no usual morning queue snaking out the door. People arrived closer to their time, the nurses had a steady flow, and the whole atmosphere was much calmer. An older passenger told me it was the first time she didn’t have to stand for an hour waiting. We’ll definitely do the roster again.

Warmly,
Enid
