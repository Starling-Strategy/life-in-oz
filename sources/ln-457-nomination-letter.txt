A Nomination for the Founders' Gala: Ren Hazelham

Format: nomination_letter
Theme: Emerald City Chamber

Cecily Orchardham:
To the Founders' Gala Nomination Committee,

I'm writing to nominate Ren Hazelham for recognition at this year's Gala, on behalf of the Emerald City Chamber. I'm Cecily Orchardham, over at the Emerald Gate Hotel. My reason is straightforward: Ren's work exemplifies the sort of quiet, daily excellence that holds a community together, and I think we should celebrate it.

My own recent experience gives me a fresh appreciation for it. Last month, during that flicker of a power outage in the Junction Quarter, our elevators were down. It was a minor scramble, but we had twelve guests needing to check out with luggage. We set up a manual log and stored everything securely in the lobby until power was restored. It was a simple, practical fix to keep things moving—the kind of thing we all do in our roles.

That's why Ren's project struck such a chord with me. I was in Hazelham Hardware a few weeks ago and noticed the plumbing section looked...unusually serene. Ren mentioned, in that dry way of his, that he'd spent a whole morning on it. He'd taken a single, chaotic bin of mixed washers and sorted them into a new drawer system, each compartment clearly labeled by millimeter diameter. No fanfare, no announcement. He just did it.

The result is that now, when a flustered homeowner comes in with a leaky tap, or a contractor needs fifty washers of a specific size, Ren—or any of his staff—can find exactly what's needed in seconds. That's more than tidiness. That's respect for other people's time and emergencies. It's a small act of order that ripples outward, making everyone's day a little smoother, a little less fraught.

In a network built on practical cooperation, Ren Hazelham's morning with the washers is a perfect model. It's the infrastructure of care, built one drawer at a time. I hope you'll agree he deserves the Chamber's nod.

Sincerely,
Cecily Orchardham
Manager, Emerald Gate Hotel
