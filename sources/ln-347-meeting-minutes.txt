Quadling Health Access Coordinating Committee
Meeting Minutes
Date: September 16, 2025
Location: Clay Hollow Community Hall

Present: Coralie Greenshaw (Chair), Gwen Rushton, Kezia Quadbourne, Kerensa Lanternstead, Wilfred Kilnley, Rowan Bell (Community Manager, ECLN).

1. Welcome and Approval of August Minutes
   The minutes from the August meeting were reviewed and approved without amendment.

2. Updates from Members
   2.1. Wilfred Kilnley reported on recent activities at Blue Orchard Care Home. He noted a recent disruption when the home's usual appliance repairer retired, leading to a delay in fixing the communal radio and music player. Residents listened to different radio programs and organized extra card games for a week until a new repair contact was established. The experience underscored the value of having a list of reliable local tradespeople for care settings.

Wilfred Kilnley:
It was a useful reminder that comfort isn't always about the big things. The card games were a hit—more conversation, less background noise. We're compiling that trades list now.

2.2. Kezia Quadbourne provided a summary of her recent 'Safe Toaster Clinic' held in Timber Verge. Seven households brought their toasters for a basic safety inspection. Kezia demonstrated how to safely unplug, cool, and clean out crumbs to prevent fires, and provided written guides.

Kezia Quadbourne:
Most were just packed with old crumbs. It's a simple thing, but a toaster fire can upend everything. Showed them how to check the cord for cracks, too. No major faults found, just a lot of needed cleaning.

2.3. Kerensa Lanternstead reported on a completed commission for the Meadowbank Apothecary. She crafted a set of three tin funnels with graduated sizes and finely rolled spouts designed for handling powdered herbs without spillage or static cling. The apothecary has confirmed the funnels are in daily use and have improved efficiency.

Kerensa Lanternstead:
The spout angle was the key. Too steep, and the powder compacts; too shallow, it drifts. We settled on a gentle curve that guides it down cleanly. They're pleased with the consistency it brings to their measures.

2.4. Gwen Rushton reported a successful coordination of patient transport. Two patients from neighbouring hamlets, both requiring thrice-weekly dialysis, now share a single transport route. This has reduced total weekly driver hours by six and cut combined fuel costs by nearly a third.

Gwen Rushton:
It required aligning their appointment times by just twenty minutes, which the clinic was able to accommodate. The patients appreciate the company for the journey, and the drivers have a more efficient schedule.

3. Planning and Proposals
   3.1. Prescription Cost Workshop (Coralie Greenshaw)
        Coralie presented a plan for a community workshop focused on navigating prescription medication costs in the Quadling region. The workshop would explain how to legally compare prices between different pharmacies, discuss generic medication options, and outline available assistance programs. The goal is to equip people with the knowledge to ask informed questions.

Coralie Greenshaw:
The price for the same bottle can vary by quite a bit from one dispensary to the next, even in neighbouring towns. People shouldn't have to choose between medication and other essentials. I'm planning to hold the first session here in Clay Hollow in late October, with printed guides.

Action: Coralie to draft workshop materials and propose a date for the October meeting.
        Support: Kezia and Gwen offered to help review the materials for clarity.

4. Resource Sharing
   The committee discussed creating a shared resource directory. Wilfred's experience and Kezia's clinic highlighted a need for a vetted list of local repair services, safety check providers, and durable medical equipment suppliers. Kerensa suggested including artisans who can make or adapt tools for specific health needs.
   Action: Rowan Bell to circulate a template for the directory. All members to contribute initial names and contacts by the next meeting.

5. Network Connections
   Rowan Bell provided a brief update on Emerald City Leadership Network activities, noting that the Transport Union's schedule coordination software might offer learnings for Gwen's transport model. Rowan will facilitate an introduction.

6. Next Meeting
   The next meeting is scheduled for October 21, 2025, at 6:00 PM in the Clay Hollow Community Hall.

Meeting adjourned at 7:45 PM.

Minutes submitted by: Rowan Bell, Community Manager
