Meeting Minutes

1. Welcome and introductions
   Rowan Bell (manager) called the meeting to order at 7:05 PM. The purpose of the meeting was to share updates on ongoing projects, coordinate resources, and plan for the upcoming season.

2. Updates from Mutual Aid Points
   Members reported on activities at their respective locations.

   a) Leif Barleycombe reported on the Tramways Lost Property Drive.
      Leif stated, 'The 60-day unclaimed period ended last week. We cataloged everything at the Brickway Junction depot. Usable items—particularly winter coats and sturdy bags—have been separated. I delivered three full cartons to the Meadowbank mutual aid group yesterday. The rest, items that were worn out or broken, went to the textile recyclers.'

   b) Phoebe Greenworth reported on the Saffron Flats Pantry coin collection.
      Phoebe said, 'The collection jars in the Can Works canteen are doing well. I've been driving the coins to the bank myself every Friday. It's a small amount each week, but it adds up and helps us buy staples in bulk. The workers there seem glad to have a way to contribute.'

   c) Kai Tinmere reported on the Reed Flats community fridge maintenance.
      Kai mentioned, 'The door seal on the main fridge started failing last Tuesday. I used heavy-duty waterproof tape as a temporary fix. It held the temperature steady until the proper replacement part arrived yesterday. The fridge never went above safe levels.'

   d) Nan Kilnholt reported on the Green Market Reading Room photo project.
      Nan reported, 'Our scanning day was quieter than expected, but we managed to help people digitize and caption over fifty old family photographs. One older gentleman brought in a picture of his grandfather standing by the original Brickway. We got the story behind it written down, too.'

3. Shared Resources and Needs
   a) Clem Kilnford shared an anecdote from the Green Market warm room.
      Clem said, 'Heard from one of the regulars that a little terrier got loose from Meadowbank last week. Apparently, it wandered into the warm room looking confused. Someone—maybe the clerk on duty—kept it calm in the back office for a couple of hours until the owner, who was frantic, showed up. Good to know the space can be a refuge for more than just people.'

   b) Saffi Wheatmore presented plans for the Heatherford Childcare Co-op.
      Saffi said, 'We're in the early stages of planning a fundraiser. We want to build a simple covered outdoor shelter so the children have a place to play when it rains or the sun is too strong. We're thinking a bake sale and a craft stall, probably in early autumn. We'll need to source materials locally—treated timber and a good waterproof fabric.'
      Discussion followed on potential collaboration. Phoebe offered to share tips on running a bake sale. Leif suggested checking with the Timber Verge Repair Co-op for material sourcing advice.
      Action: Saffi to draft a plan and budget for the fundraiser and shelter by the next meeting.

4. Coordination and Logistics
   a) Transport
      Leif noted that the Tramways union might be able to assist with moving larger donations if given sufficient notice.
   b) Communication
      Nan suggested using the Reading Room noticeboards to post updates about pantry needs and community events. All agreed.
   c) Temperature-Sensitive Goods
      Kai reminded the group that the community fridge in Reed Flats has some surplus capacity and can accept perishable donations from other areas if coordinated in advance.

5. Next Steps and Future Meetings
   - The next meeting is scheduled for September 16, 2026, at 7:00 PM, location to be confirmed.
   - Rowan Bell (manager) will circulate a summary of these minutes.
   - Members are to report any urgent needs or changes in capacity to the group via the network's message board.

6. Adjournment
   The meeting was adjourned at 8:20 PM.
