Emerald City Leadership Network Membership Application

Applicant Name: Ida Poppyford

Organization: Poppyford Design Studio

Date: January 2024

Primary Circle Interest: Young Founders

Please describe a recent project where you collaborated with or contributed to a community group.

Ida Poppyford:
Two things come to mind right away! First, I made a workbook for the Young Founders workshop on pricing. All those cost structures and profit margins can glaze eyes over, so I drew up flowcharts and little visual metaphors—like a watering can for overhead costs—to make it stick. They used it last month and said the room was a lot less quiet.

Please describe a second project or contribution.

Ida Poppyford:
Oh, the other one was the banners for the Festival Committee. They needed something for the street fair that wouldn’t wilt in a downpour and could change a bit each year without starting from scratch. I sewed up these big fabric panels with a clever grommet system—the center artwork pops out. They’re up on Lantern Row right now, looking bright even after that last windy night.

What do you hope to gain from joining the network?

Ida Poppyford:
Connections, honestly. I can make things clear and colorful, but I want to meet the people who are doing the work—the growers, the transit folks, the repair co-ops. See where my studio can slot in.
