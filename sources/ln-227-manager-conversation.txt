Rowan Bell — community manager:
Called the three Winkie Works Guild members to check in on projects and plans. Iolo, Kezia, and Hazel all joined.

Iolo Winkleton:
Iolo said he’d finished walking the Saffron Flats floor and made a list. Counted seventeen mats with the tread worn smooth or corners curled. Said it’s a tripping hazard and bad on the knees. Got the Guild’s approval to replace them in batches—first six ordered, should be in by end of the month.

Kezia Meadowshaw:
Kezia mentioned a neighbor’s garden shed door in Yellow Kiln. Said the frame had warped so bad it wouldn’t close. She rebuilt it with pressure-treated lumber from her shop and used heavy-duty hinges she got through the Guild’s supplier list. Said it swings true now.

Hazel Copperstead:
Hazel said she’s drafting a proposal for a Guild-wide safety day focused on apprentices. It would cover ladder setup and electrical hazard recognition, hands-on. Said she’s talking to the Guild’s training committee about funding for materials and instructor time. No date set yet; it’s still in planning.

Iolo Winkleton:
Iolo agreed the safety day is a good idea. Said apprentices at the Can Works could use a refresher on lock-out tag-out, but that’s a separate thing.

Kezia Meadowshaw:
Kezia said she’d be willing to help with the ladder station if the day happens, but only if it’s scheduled outside her busy season.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Hazel noted the interest and said she’d keep them posted as the proposal develops.
