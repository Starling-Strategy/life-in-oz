Rowan Bell — community manager:
Post-event debrief: Small Business Circle, October 2024.

Attended: Mattie Amberstead, Jasper Brookwood, Mabel Brambleshaw, Bram Meadowholt, Rowan Bell.

Aim was to touch base, share a win or a current focus, and see if the network could connect anyone to a resource or contact.

Mattie brought up the community lunch in Reed Flats. She said Gillikin Growers had a shipment of sunflowers that were just about to go, so she got them at a good rate. She made two dozen arrangements in mason jars, kept them simple with just the sunflowers and some trailing greenery. Said the idea was something cheerful nobody had to fuss over; they could just take the jar home. She mentioned that a couple of the older folks who came to the lunch seemed really pleased, which made the morning feel worthwhile.

Jasper's update was about the back office. He said his accountant has been after him for over a year to move off his notebook system and onto a proper software. He's decided next Tuesday morning, before the cafe opens, he's going to sit down with it and just start. He asked if anyone else uses 'accounting software' specifically. Bram said he's familiar with the platform and offered to be a sounding board if Jasper hits a confusing screen. Jasper seemed relieved, said he might take him up on that.

Mabel described a tricky delivery last week. A client in the Quadling marshes needed a full load of those tall, clear greenhouse panels for a repair job, and the delivery window was tight because of an incoming weather system. The load was oversize for a single truck under the union's road rules. She coordinated with another driver from the Brickway Transit Union, split the load between her rig and his, and they ran the route in tandem. Got both trucks unloaded at the site with about half a day to spare before the rain started. She said the dispatcher was happy, the client was happy, and she got to share the long drive with someone, which made a change.

Bram quietly mentioned he'd just finished up with the Timber Verge Community Chorus's books. They'd gotten behind, about six months' worth of bank statements needed reconciling. He found three donations that had been recorded in the wrong fund—someone had handwritten 'general' when the donor specified 'new music fund.' He corrected the entries and gave them a clean summary sheet. Said the chorus treasurer was very grateful; they'd been trying to untangle it themselves for a while.

General discussion afterward touched on the upcoming Meadow Mutual Aid winter coat drive (Mattie offered to be a drop-off point) and the rising cost of flour (Jasper's perennial concern). No direct requests for network help emerged from this session, but the offer of software advice between Bram and Jasper was a good, spontaneous connection.

Actions for me (Rowan): None from this session, but follow up with Jasper in a couple of weeks to see how the software setup went.
