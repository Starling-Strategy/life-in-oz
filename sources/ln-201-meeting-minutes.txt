Wizard's Office Public Works Committee
Meeting Date: March 2025
Present: Committee Chair; Owen Flint, Drainage Inspector; Mott Peppercombe, Bridge Crew Lead; Leona Bracken (Bracken Freight); a representative of the Quadling Freight Association; Rowan Bell, Community Manager (Emerald City Leadership Network).

1. Approval of Previous Minutes
   The minutes from the February meeting were approved as circulated.

2. Public Comment
   No members of the public were present.

3. Drainage Inspection Report – Mile 14 Crossing
   Owen Flint presented his findings. He stated, 'I inspected the south footing of the Mile 14 crossing on March 10th. There is significant scour under the foundation. I have photographs showing the erosion.' He distributed copies of the photographs to the committee. No immediate structural failure was noted, but the condition was classified as requiring monitoring and remediation.

Leona Bracken:
4. Member Presentation – Safety Concerns at Mile 14 Crossing
   Leona Bracken of Bracken Freight was invited to speak. She began, 'I'm a bit nervous saying this, but it needs saying. I drive that route. So do my drivers. The deck on that crossing is failing. Every heavy truck that goes over it is a risk.' She presented her own set of photographs showing cracked deck planks and deteriorated railings. 'I'm asking, officially, that the crossing be closed to loaded trucks before we get into another hauling season. An empty rig is one thing, but a loaded one? It's not a matter of if, it's when.'

The representative of the Quadling Freight Association objected, stating that closure would add significant time and cost to deliveries for several local growers and the Brickway Transit Union's supply runs. They argued for a weight limit instead of a full closure.

Leona Bracken:
Leona Bracken responded, 'A weight limit sounds good on paper, but who's going to enforce it at midnight? A sign won't stop a desperate driver or a new hire. The risk is plain. We close it to trucks, we fix it, then we reopen. The alternative is a truck in the creek.'

After discussion, and considering Owen Flint's report on the scour, the committee agreed to close the Mile 14 crossing to all truck traffic exceeding two axles, effective April 1, 2025. Signage will be posted at both approaches. Passenger vehicles, wagons, and light carts may continue to use the crossing.

5. Action Items
   a. The Wizard's Office signage department will produce and install 'No Trucks' and weight restriction signs by March 31.
   b. Mott Peppercombe's bridge crew from Brickway Works is tasked with installing physical truck barriers (post-and-cable system) at the crossing during the first week of April. This work is scheduled but not yet begun.
   c. Owen Flint will conduct a follow-up inspection after the spring runoff and provide a report at the May meeting to inform repair planning.

6. Next Meeting
   The next committee meeting is scheduled for April 25, 2025.

Minutes submitted by the Committee Clerk.
