Emerald Gate Hotel Announces Cost Savings and Expanded Staff Transit Access

Format: press_release
Theme: Emerald Gate Hotel

FOR RELEASE
Emerald City, Oz – November 2025 – The Emerald Gate Hotel today announced two new operational initiatives, a cost-saving contract with a local vendor and a transportation program for overnight employees, both spearheaded by Hotel Manager Cecily Orchardham.

The first initiative is a new agreement with a Munchkinland-based linen and uniform service. After a review of vendor contracts, Orchardham successfully renegotiated terms, securing a fifteen percent reduction in the cost of laundering staff uniforms for the hotel. The savings are expected to be reinvested into employee amenities.

Cecily Orchardham:
It was a straightforward conversation about volume and long-term partnership. We both understood the value of keeping business local and reliable. This reduction helps our bottom line without compromising the quality our team deserves.

The second measure addresses a longstanding challenge for staff working the late shift. With the last scheduled Emerald City tram departing before many night-shift workers finish, Orchardham arranged for the hotel to provide shared-access bus passes. These passes are available to any overnight employee needing transport home after public transit has ended for the evening.

Cecily Orchardham:
It’s a basic matter of safety and respect. If someone is here cleaning rooms or managing the front desk until two in the morning, we have a responsibility to ensure they can get home. The shared passes are a practical solution we’re pleased to offer.

Both changes are now in effect. The Emerald Gate Hotel, located near the city's Eastern Gate, is a member of the Emerald City Chamber within the Emerald City Leadership Network.
