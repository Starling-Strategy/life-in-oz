Rowan Bell — community manager:
Rowan called Yara Wheatley for a regular check-in. Yara was cheerful, said the shop was steady.

Yara Wheatley:
Oh, Rowan, you won't believe the week we've had! First, an accountant from over on the Winkie road–a neighbor, I think the name was–came in looking absolutely frantic. His neighbor told me later his whole shipment of ledger books got held up by that mudslide near Yellow Clay. He needed a whole case, standard green binding, by the next morning for a client audit.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Rowan asked if Yara was able to help.

Yara Wheatley:
Well, of course! I had the stock. It's just sitting there, and his business was on the line. I told him to take what he needed and settle up when his own shipment finally arrives. No sense in a good ledger going unused while someone's drowning in paperwork.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Rowan noted the second item, about the Chamber order.

Yara Wheatley:
Right! That was the other big thing. The Emerald City Chamber placed an order for two hundred custom notebooks for their regional meeting. They wanted the network's emblem–the one with the compass and the gate–embossed in silver on the cover. We got the proof approved in a day, ran the press for two full shifts, and had them boxed and delivered to the Civic Hall by the seventh. The chamber clerk sent a lovely note; she said they looked even better than she'd hoped.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Rowan thanked Yara for the update and confirmed the next check-in would be in April.
