Organization/Profession: Barber, Quadwood's Barbers, Yellow Kiln (Winkie Country)

Why are you interested in joining the network?

Aurelia Quadwood:
Heard about it from a regular who drives for the Brickway Transit Union. Says it's good for finding out how other folks are solving problems. My shop's always been a bit of a hub, and I'd like to see how that can do more for the neighborhood.

Please describe a recent initiative or project you've been involved in.

Aurelia Quadwood:
Started putting ceramics from a local potter in my front window last fall. A new piece every month. It brightens the place up, gives them a little showcase. I've noticed a few more people pausing to look in, which is nice for both of us.

Are there any community-focused activities you are currently planning?

Aurelia Quadwood:
Yes. Planning a winter coat mending day for when the cold sets in. Folks can bring in a coat that needs a button or a seam fixed while they wait for a trim. Got the thread and extra needles. Figure if you're sitting here anyway, might as well get two things sorted.

What do you hope to gain or contribute through network membership?

Aurelia Quadwood:
Hoping to hear how other small shops manage things. Maybe find a better supplier for linens. And if anyone's run a similar mending day, I'd like to know what they learned. I can offer a chair and a listening ear to anyone passing through Yellow Kiln.
