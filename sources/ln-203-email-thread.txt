My addition: In late February, we had that heavy rain come through right after I'd started a tray of Black Gillikin tomatoes. The small greenhouse had developed a hairline crack in one of the upper panels—likely from the winter freeze-thaw. I used a clear adhesive patch from the library's mending kit. Sealed it up just before the storm. Humidity held steady at the right level; the seeds never got waterlogged. Simple fix, but it kept the batch viable. Labeled the patch in the log: "Panel 4, repaired 28 Feb, adhesive type B."
