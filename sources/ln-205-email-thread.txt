Martin Woodward:
From: Martin Woodward, Woodward Timber & Trail
To: Western Route road crew
Subject: Western Route open to Timber Verge
Date: March 21, 2025

All,

The Western Route is open from the river crossing to Timber Verge. We had three days with the chainsaw crew clearing what the storm brought down, about sixty trees across the road, two of them oaks.

The relief trucks got through at four this afternoon. Drivers should keep to the uphill lane past the old clearing until the bank is shored up.

Photographs of the cleared sections are attached: mile 2, mile 5 and the clearing.

Martin

Good work. The bank past the old clearing is ours. I've looked at it from the road and it wants proper shoring, not sandbags. My crew can start on it next week once the stone is delivered. Until then, uphill lane only, as you say.

Kerensa
