All,

Two items for the record.

First, the tool loan program is now active. We’ve assembled twelve starter kits—each with a framing hammer, a combination square, a two-foot level, and a tape measure—and they’re available for any apprentice in good standing to sign out for up to six months. The idea is to get a quality tool in their hands without the upfront cost. Already have three kits out, and the feedback is they’re holding up to daily use just fine.

Second, I’ve placed young a neighbor (the one who showed such a keen eye for stone) with Master Carver one of the aides at the Brickway Junction worksite. We confirmed she has a fitted hard hat, gloves, and hearing protection before she started. one of the aides and I also sat down and drafted a clear set of goals for her first month: she’ll be learning to read a cutting diagram, practicing basic dressing techniques on scrap, and observing the setting of cornerstones. She started on Monday and reports it’s going well.

That’s all for now.
