All,

Got the bakery mixer running again. It's the big cast-iron one from the old Reed Flats bakery. The motor was completely seized and the drive pin had sheared off. Found a scrap of brass rod in the bin, turned it down to 3mm, and machined a new pin. Re-greased the gears and it’s back in service. The baker says it’s quieter now than it was before it broke.

Mott,

That’s good work with the brass. It’ll hold up better than steel for that application, less wear on the softer gear.

I had the rocking chair come in from the South Meadow donation pile. The wooden runners were worn nearly flat on the bottoms, so it just sort of… shivered. Took the old ones off completely. Found a nice piece of ash in the shop. You have to draw the curve just right—not too steep, not too shallow—and then cut it so the grain follows the arc for strength. I fitted them with mortise and tenon joints, same as the original, no screws. Took two days for the glue to set properly. She rocks like a boat on a calm river now.

Arthur
