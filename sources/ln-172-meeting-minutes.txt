Festival Committee – Emerald City
Meeting Date: February 12, 2025
Present: Lorna Plumshaw (Volunteer Lead), Aurelia Ashmere (School Librarian), Lottie Wren (Puppet Maker), Linus Barleyfield (Musician), Rowan Bell (Community Manager)

1. Welcome and Approval of January Minutes
   Minutes from the January meeting were approved as circulated.

2. Volunteer Coordination Update (Lorna Plumshaw)
   Lorna reported on inventory maintenance. She noted that a canvas bag used for transporting marquee pegs had torn under the weight of the metal hardware. She took it home, repaired the split seam using waxed thread on her personal sewing machine, and has returned it to the storage shed. The repair is holding well and the bag is ready for the next windy-day setup.

3. Outreach and Promotions Report (Aurelia Ashmere)
   Aurelia distributed 500 bookmarks she designed and printed, featuring key festival dates and locations. These have been placed in local libraries and community centers across the Emerald City quarters. She received positive feedback from a librarian in Junction Quarter, who said the bookmarks were being taken quickly.

4. Puppetry and Props Status (Lottie Wren)
   Lottie provided an update on the large animal puppets. The giraffe frame had begun to collapse after last season’s processions. She has reinforced the internal structure with additional aluminum struts, a fix she described as 'more about support than replacement.' The puppet has since been used successfully in three smaller parades and is now considered stable for the main festival processions.

5. New Business: Proposal for Neighborhood Concerts (Linus Barleyfield)
   Linus presented a proposal for expanding musical offerings. 'The band’s been practicing some new pieces,' he said, 'and we’re thinking, why keep the music in the band hall? Let’s take it to the parks.' He proposed organizing a series of short, free afternoon concerts in different neighborhood parks leading up to the festival. He suggested involving other local musical groups to create a 'rotating repertoire' across the city. 'We could start in Spectacle Lane Park, then maybe move to Junction Quarter Green—give each neighborhood its own little overture,' he added.

6. Discussion of Concert Proposal
   Aurelia asked about scheduling conflicts with library events. Lorna inquired about logistics: who would handle permits, sound equipment, and rain plans. Lottie suggested the concerts could be paired with simple puppet demonstrations for families. Linus acknowledged the questions and said his plan was still in the 'first draft' stage. He offered to draft a more detailed plan, including a proposed schedule and list of potential partner groups, for the next meeting.

7. Action Items
   - Linus Barleyfield to draft a detailed proposal for neighborhood park concerts, including a tentative schedule, permit requirements, and potential partner groups. (Due: March committee meeting)
   - Lorna Plumshaw to conduct a full inventory check of all storage sheds and report any additional repairs needed by the end of February.
   - Aurelia Ashmere to follow up with community centers in Winkie and Gillikin countries regarding bookmark distribution.
   - Lottie Wren to assess the remaining large puppet frames and provide a maintenance estimate by the March meeting.

8. Next Meeting
   The next Festival Committee meeting is scheduled for March 12, 2025, at 6:00 PM in the Emerald City Chamber meeting room.

Meeting adjourned at 7:45 PM.
