Hattie Violetholt:
At Westgate Foundry, we’ve started using a new staged cooling procedure for our large iron molds. Instead of pulling them from the sand right away and letting them air-cool, we’re now keeping them insulated longer and managing the temperature drop over a full day. The result is we’ve seen a drop in thermal stress cracks along the seams of our heavier pieces. The castings are coming out more consistent.

Magnus Emeryham:
Fabricated a dozen custom brackets for the new disabled access ramp at the Tinsmith Row Community Hall. The original specs weren’t holding. Designed these to interface with the existing stonework and meet the updated safety code for lateral load. Installed last Tuesday.

Kezia Meadowshaw:
Saw the benches in the park by the kiln are in a state. Spoke to the town clerk. I’ve offered to replace the broken slats. Just waiting on the municipal work order approval and for them to get the lumber delivered. Shouldn’t take more than a morning once it’s here.

Hazel Copperstead:
Had a sit-down this week with one of our carpentry apprentices and their master. The apprentice was getting frustrated with dovetails, and the master was concerned about pace. We went back to the bench, looked at the work, and agreed on a revised plan: two weeks focusing solely on lap and bridle joints to rebuild confidence in the fundamentals. It was a good talk.
