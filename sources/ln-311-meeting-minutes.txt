Quadling Health Access Coordinating Committee Minutes
Date: August 2025
Present: Rowan Bell (Community Manager, Emerald City Leadership Network), Wilhelmina Thistlewaite (Clinic manager, Bluebell Crossing Clinic), Odile Wickley (Health access navigator, Quadling Health Access), Orla Amberholt (Road crew foreman, Brickway Works), Mara Wells (Care equipment loan coordinator, North Orchard loan desk).

1. Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 3:00 PM by Rowan Bell.

2. Updates from Members
2.1 Wilhelmina Thistlewaite (Billie) reported on her efforts regarding the Bluebell Crossing Clinic's communication systems. She is currently circulating a petition among local businesses in Bluebell Crossing and the surrounding Munchkin County townships. The petition aims to demonstrate community support for a grant application to fund a digital referral system. The goal is to replace the clinic's aging and unreliable fax machine, which frequently drops transmissions of patient referrals. Billie stated she has secured signatures from three local grocers and the owner of the Bluebell Inn so far.

2.2 Odile Wickley provided an update on her resource list project. She has completed a simple, updated list of pediatric dentists in the Emerald City and western Gillikin who offer income-based sliding scale fees. The list includes the practices' current contact information, eligibility requirements, and typical appointment lead times. She printed fifty copies for distribution at the North Orchard community center and the Quadling Health Access office in Redfern Hill. Initial feedback from two families last week confirmed that the information helped them schedule consultations.

2.3 Orla Amberholt reported on Brickway Works crew logistics. She is in the process of arranging a service agreement with a local industrial laundry in Redfern Hill. The plan is for the laundry to clean the road crew's soiled, high-visibility reflective vests on a monthly schedule. Orla stated this is intended to improve the vests' visibility for crew safety and to address crew concerns about wearing dirty gear, which she believes impacts morale. She is gathering quotes and will present a proposal to the Brickway Works administrative lead next week.

2.4 Mara Wells reported on inventory work at the North Orchard loan desk. She received a donation of mixed walking aids from an estate clearance. Over three days last week, she sorted through the collection, inspecting each item. She identified and tagged twelve adjustable aluminum canes and four four-wheeled rollators that met the loan desk's safety standards. These items have been moved to the cleaning station and are awaiting sterilization before being added to the active loan inventory.

3. Discussion and Coordination
3.1 Rowan Bell suggested Odile share a copy of the pediatric dentist list with Billie for the Bluebell Crossing Clinic's waiting room. Odile agreed to mail five copies.
3.2 Mara Wells asked if the industrial laundry service Orla is pursuing might be suitable for cleaning loaned medical equipment linens. Orla said she would inquire about capacity and rates for non-vest items and report back.
3.3 The group discussed the challenge of transporting equipment from the Quadling country to Gillikin for repairs. No immediate solution was identified, but Rowan agreed to raise it at the next Network transit circle meeting.

4. Action Items
- Wilhelmina Thistlewaite: Continue gathering petition signatures; aim for twenty by end of September.
- Odile Wickley: Mail five copies of the pediatric dentist list to Bluebell Crossing Clinic.
- Orla Amberholt: Finalize laundry service quotes and present proposal to Brickway Works admin.
- Mara Wells: Oversee the cleaning and intake process for the twelve canes and four rollators.
- Rowan Bell: Inquire about cross-country transport for medical equipment at the transit circle.

5. Next Meeting
The next meeting is scheduled for October 2025.

Meeting adjourned at 4:10 PM.
