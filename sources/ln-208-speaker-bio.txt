Merritt Thistleshaw serves as Membership Officer for the Emerald City Chamber and is a resident of Bell Market. Her work focuses on making the Chamber's resources accessible and responsive to the city's small businesses. Recently, she successfully persuaded three Bell Market produce stallholders to join by outlining the practical benefits of a trial membership, particularly the bulk purchasing discounts available on essential items like cardboard boxes and cleaning supplies. In a separate effort last month, Merritt tracked down a full set of new member welcome packets that had been misdelivered to a vacant office suite adjacent to the Chamber's headquarters. Her prompt retrieval ensured the packets reached their intended recipients before the new businesses had a chance to feel overlooked by the association. Merritt brings a warm, efficient approach to her role, grounded in the day-to-day realities of market commerce.
