All,

A quick update: the closing dance school over on Lullaby Lane was clearing out. I was able to secure their large rehearsal mirror—it’s a bit tarnished on the silvering at the very edges, but perfectly serviceable. Had one of our carpenters reinforce the wooden frame, and we mounted it in the practice space yesterday afternoon. It’s up and stable. This saves us the entire budget line for a new one.

Let me know if the angle needs any adjustment.

From: Marlo Kilncombe
To: Hammerhead Rise Festival Volunteers
Date: 5 May 2025
Subject: Decorations are up!

Hello everyone,

Just a note to say the main square is looking wonderfully festive! I connected with the Stitch & Mend collective over in the southern marshes and sourced several long strings of colourful bunting made entirely from their recycled fabric scraps. The cost was a fraction of new materials, and the effect is perfect. It’s all strung up between the lantern posts now. Do take a look if you’re passing through.

Cheers,
Marlo
