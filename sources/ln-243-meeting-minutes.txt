Winkie Works Guild
Monthly Business Meeting
May 2025

Present: Tilda Poppyley, Reuben Brookcombe, Lena Kilnfield, Rowan Bell (Community Manager, non-voting).

1. Call to Order
Meeting called to order at 7:05 PM. No reading of previous minutes as copies were circulated via the Yellow Brick Board.

2. Recent Member Projects
Members shared updates on recent hands-on work.

   a) Tilda Poppyley reported on a fabrication project completed last month. A Winkie community greenhouse required a wide rainwater collection gutter. She formed a continuous channel from a single roll of salvaged galvanized steel, eliminating seams and potential leak points. The gutter is now installed and functioning.

   b) Reuben Brookcombe described a repair for a small grain mill in the western Winkie Country. The mill's sorting conveyor belt had a broken pulley wheel. Reuben machined a custom-sized replacement from stock on hand. From receiving the call to having the mill operational again took two days.

   c) Lena Kilnfield shared a small repair completed at the Gate Quarter school library. The book return cart had a cracked caster, making it difficult to move. She identified a compatible spare part from a broken office chair slated for disposal. The cart is now back in service, and the school avoided a maintenance call-out fee.

3. Discussion: Skills-Sharing Session Proposal
Rowan Bell proposed the Guild consider hosting a practical skills-sharing session for the wider network later in the year. The idea would be to demonstrate basic metalworking, simple machining, and repair techniques applicable to small businesses and community spaces.

   - Reuben Brookcombe stated he would be willing to demonstrate basic lathe safety and how to turn a simple bushing, provided the session was kept small for safety.
   - Tilda Poppyley agreed in principle but emphasized the need for a very clear scope and proper liability waivers if anyone other than members were to handle tools.
   - Lena Kilnfield suggested pairing demonstrations with a 'repair clinic' format, where people could bring a single, small broken item for assessment, not necessarily for on-the-spot repair. This could help manage expectations and volume.

4. Action Items
   - ACTION (Rowan Bell): To draft a one-page proposal for a skills-sharing session, incorporating the repair clinic idea and liability considerations, for review at the June meeting.
   - ACTION (All Members): To think of 2-3 specific, safe, and beginner-appropriate tasks that could be demonstrated in a 20-minute slot.

5. Other Business
   - Reuben raised the issue of consistent supply for certain tool steel grades. Tilda noted a supplier in Timber Verge that she uses. They agreed to exchange contact details after the meeting.
   - Lena asked if the Guild had a small budget for a shared reference library, such as manuals or material spec sheets. Rowan will investigate if network micro-grant funds could be applied for this purpose and report back.

6. Next Meeting
Scheduled for June 10, 2025, at 7:00 PM at the Westgate Foundry meeting room.

Meeting adjourned at 8:20 PM.
