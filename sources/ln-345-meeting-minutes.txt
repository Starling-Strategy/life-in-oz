Wizard's Office Liaisons Committee
Meeting Minutes
Date: 12 September 2025
Present: Heath Gatecombe, Abel Larkwood, Orla Greenwick, Wynn Thistlewick, Rowan Bell (Community Manager)

1. Welcome and Introductions
Rowan Bell opened the meeting, noting the agenda would focus on recent field updates and coordination ahead of the autumn season.

2. Field Reports from Ward Councillors
Heath Gatecombe reported on a resolved neighborhood dispute in Brickway Junction. Two neighbors were experiencing basement damp due to garden runoff from a shared property line. Heath mediated between them, inspecting the site and proposing a shared gravel trench to redirect water. Both parties accepted the solution and have agreed to share the cost of materials. Work is scheduled to begin next week.

Abel Larkwood reported on a proactive maintenance effort in the Bell Market ward's residential lanes. Following the early autumn leaf drop, he coordinated a weekend volunteer crew of six residents to clear clogged storm drains along a residential lane, preventing potential basement flooding for several homes. The crew filled eight bags with wet leaves and sediment.

3. Updates from Wizard's Office Staff
Orla Greenwick provided an update on seasonal preparations. In anticipation of a forecasted heavy snowfall, she has drafted and distributed a public notice outlining the schedule and priority routes for Emerald City street clearing. The notice was posted at civic buildings, distributed to the Reading Room Network, and will run in the Emerald City Gazette for two weeks prior to the expected weather.

Wynn Thistlewick reported on a recently processed grant. The Meadowbank After-School Club in Munchkin Country applied for a minor equipment grant to replace a burnt-out projector bulb ahead of their end-of-term film showing. Wynn expedited the review, and the club received the funds within three days, allowing them to purchase the bulb in time for the event.

4. Discussion: Coordination for Autumn and Winter
   a. Drainage and Flood Prevention: Heath and Abel suggested compiling a brief, shared guide for residents on simple gutter and drain maintenance, to be distributed via public notices and community boards. Orla agreed to include a reference to such a guide in future seasonal notices.
   b. Grant Accessibility: Wynn noted that the expedited process for very small grants (under fifty silver pieces) has proven effective and asked liaisons to encourage small community groups to apply without hesitation. Abel will mention this at the next Bell Market community meeting.
   c. Communication Channels: Orla reminded the group that all official scheduling notices will originate from her office, but ward councillors are encouraged to reinforce the information through their local networks to ensure wide reach.

5. Action Items
   - Heath Gatecombe: To follow up on the gravel trench installation and report any issues at the next meeting.
   - Abel Larkwood: To promote the minor grant program at the next Bell Market community meeting (scheduled for late September).
   - Orla Greenwick: To liaise with Heath and Abel on the draft drainage maintenance guide and determine the best method for its dissemination.
   - Wynn Thistlewick: To share a simplified one-page summary of the minor grant application process with all committee members for their reference.
   - Rowan Bell: To circulate these minutes and add the drainage guide project to the October agenda for review.

6. Next Meeting
Scheduled for 10 October 2025, 3pm, at the Wizard's Office Annex (Gate Quarter).

Meeting adjourned at 4:15pm.
