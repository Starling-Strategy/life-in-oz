FOR RELEASE
November 2024
CLAY HOLLOW, Quadling Country – Kezia Quadbourne of the Timber Verge Repair Co-op recently demonstrated that a keen ear can be as valuable as any tool. According to reports from her workshop, a client brought in a silent refrigerator, concerned it was beyond repair. Quadbourne listened closely to the unit's faint, irregular hum and correctly diagnosed a failing compressor before any disassembly was required.

Kezia Quadbourne:
Most folks hear 'silent' and think the worst. But that little hum was telling a story—it was struggling, not dead. We ordered the right part straight away, and the owner saved the time and fee for a full diagnostic teardown.

In a separate initiative to improve the co-op's shared workspace, Quadbourne has drafted plans for wall-mounted shelving to better organize tools and parts. The project, however, is currently on hold.

Kezia Quadbourne:
The plans are drawn. It’s a simple, solid design that’ll get everything off the floor and within reach. But we’re waiting on a donation of the right timber—something sturdy enough to hold the weight. We’ll build it as soon as the materials come through.

The Timber Verge Repair Co-op, a member of the Emerald City Leadership Network, provides appliance repair and maintenance services across the Clay Hollow area, emphasizing skill-sharing and community resourcefulness.
