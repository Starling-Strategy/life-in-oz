Emerald City Leadership Network – Members' Survey – May 2024

Question 1: What's a recent challenge you navigated at work, and how did you handle it?

Mabel Brambleshaw:
The Brickway bridge at Tallow Creek was closed for repairs last week. Had a pallet of kiln parts for the pottery co-op in Blue Orchard, due that afternoon. Main route was a hard stop. I remembered an old farm service road that cuts behind the Glimmerfield orchards—bumpy as all get-out, but it’s solid ground and comes out past the closure. Took it slow, added an hour, but the parts got there on time. Clerk at the co-op said they’d have had to halt three firings without them.

Question 2: Are there any small changes you're considering to make your daily work easier or safer?

Mabel Brambleshaw:
Seat cushions. These long runs to Gillikin and back, my back knows every mile. The stock seat’s shot. I’m looking into getting a custom cushion made—one of the upholsterers in the Emerald City market does them for truckers. Measured the cab last week. It’s not cheap, but neither is a week laid up. Just figuring out the fill; need support but it can’t be stone.

Question 3: Is there anything the network or your circle could do to support your work?

Mabel Brambleshaw:
Mostly just hearing about these detours and closures sooner. Someone usually knows before the signs go up.
