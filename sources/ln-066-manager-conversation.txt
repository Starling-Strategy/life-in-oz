Rowan Bell — community manager:
Joint check-in with Yara and Ursa. Both described recent projects.

Yara Wheatley:
Rowan, I’ve been wanting to tell you about this new artist I found in Violet Hills. She makes these incredible pressed botanical prints on handmade paper—ferns, poppy field wildflowers, things like that. The paper has this wonderful texture. I started carrying a small line last month, and it’s been a steady seller with the summer tourists. They like having a piece of the countryside to take home. I’m thinking of expanding the selection if she can keep up with the pressing.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Ursa, how are things at the library?

Ursa Saffronridge:
Quite well. I noticed a particular group of students who seemed to dread the free period because the cafeteria is so loud. So, I set up a 'silent reading afternoon' in a corner of the library. I arranged some comfortable floor cushions, and I managed to get a set of booklights from the supply closet. It’s not a large group, maybe six or seven, but they come every week now. It’s lovely to see them find a quiet spot.

Yara Wheatley:
Oh, that’s wonderful. A quiet corner is a precious thing. Maybe I should send some bookmarks your way.

Ursa Saffronridge:
That would be very kind, Yara. The students would appreciate that.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Thanks, both. Good to hear these are going. No immediate support needed?

Not at the moment, just pleased it’s working.

All is well here, thank you.
