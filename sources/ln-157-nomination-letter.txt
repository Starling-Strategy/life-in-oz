Agnes Fernton:
To the Founders' Gala Committee,

I’m writing to nominate Xan Cinderby for recognition at this year’s Gala. While our work in the Quadling Health Access circle and Xan’s in the Small Business Circle might seem like different worlds, I’ve seen firsthand how the same kind of careful, structured thinking can build something durable for the community—something that quietly makes things work better for everyone.

My own recent effort was digging through old files at the Green Market Clinic to find a grant template for the community pool ramp. It was an afternoon in a dusty storage room, but having that proven framework meant we could reapply for funding with confidence, not guesswork. It’s that same principle of creating a reliable template for a common problem that makes Xan’s contribution so valuable.

Xan didn’t just run tutoring sessions. She identified that students across the city were hitting the same walls—fractions, word problems—and she built a set of modular workbooks to address those specific stumbling blocks. Now her tutors at Cinderby Tutoring have a consistent, high-quality resource to supplement their teaching. It’s a scalable solution that improves outcomes without requiring each tutor to reinvent the wheel every time.

This is the sort of foundational work that strengthens our whole network. It’s practical, it’s thoughtful, and it frees up other people’s energy to focus on their students, or in my case, their patients. Xan has built a tool that empowers others to do their best work, and that’s precisely the spirit the Founders’ Gala should celebrate.

Sincerely,
Agnes Fernton
Quadling Health Access Circle
