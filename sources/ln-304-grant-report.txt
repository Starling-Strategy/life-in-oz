Wizard's Office Liaisons Small Grant Final Report – August 2025

Format: grant_report
Theme: Wizard's Office Liaisons

FOR RELEASE: August 2025, Emerald City

Grant Recipient: Emerald City Leadership Network, Wizard's Office Liaisons Circle
Grant Period: June – August 2025
Grant Purpose: To support member-led projects improving the flow of information and access between community organizations and the Wizard's Office administration.

REPORT SUMMARY
The allocated funds were used to acquire specialized archival supplies and to cover communication expenses for two liaison members, enabling the preservation of vital civic records and the facilitation of critical health-access navigation. The work demonstrates the tangible value of sustained, person-to-person connection between civic administration and the communities it serves.

PROJECT DETAILS
Funds supported two distinct efforts led by Liaisons Circle members.

Edmund Tinworth, a records clerk at the Wizard's Office, addressed a preservation crisis. In early July, a minor leak in a records-storage basement threatened several years' worth of property permits and land-use filings. Using grant funds, Tinworth procured acid-free blotting paper, document drying racks, and climate-controlled storage containers. He meticulously sorted and air-dried each water-damaged page, a process that took the better part of three weeks. His precise work saved the documents from mold and irreversible damage, preserving a complete chain of records for the Emerald City Planning Commission's upcoming review cycle.

Coralie Greenshaw, a health access navigator with Quadling Health Access, applied the grant to maintain robust communication channels. Clinic staff in Purplemoor report that Greenshaw's persistence is legendary. In one recent instance, staff say she tracked down a missing application for a hearing aid repair subsidy for an elderly client by placing calls to four different Wizard's Office departments in a single morning. The grant covered the costs associated with these sustained outreach efforts, ensuring that navigators like Greenshaw can bridge gaps without straining their own programs' limited budgets.

BUDGET ALLOCATION
- Archival Preservation Supplies (blotting paper, drying racks, storage): 60%
- Communication Expenses (dedicated line costs, call credits): 35%
- Administrative & Reporting: 5%

UNFINISHED WORK / NEXT STEPS
The success of the preservation project has highlighted the need for a broader, office-wide disaster preparedness protocol for physical records. The Liaisons Circle intends to draft a proposal for such a protocol, but this work falls outside the scope of the completed grant. Similarly, the model of dedicated communication support for navigators requires longer-term funding to become a permanent fixture.

CONCLUSION
This grant has proven that small, targeted investments in the individuals who form the connective tissue between institutions and the public yield significant, concrete returns: saved history and restored access.
