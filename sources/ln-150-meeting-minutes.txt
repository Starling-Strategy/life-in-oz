Gillikin Growers Cooperative
Monthly Coordination Meeting
January 27, 2025

Present: Beryl Brookford (Gillikin Growers Cooperative), Nico Poppycombe (Meadow Mutual Aid), Ivy Hedgeton (Gillikin Growers), Pia Reedford (Tinsmith Row Grain Mill), Iolo Ashcombe (freelance translator), Rowan Bell (Network Community Manager)

1. Logistics & Freight Consolidation (Item led by Beryl Brookford)
Beryl reported on a recent consolidation effort. Three partial truckloads of potatoes from farms in the Violet Hills area were combined into a single shipment destined for a food processor in Quadling Country. The cooperative utilized an empty return trailer from a member who had delivered a load of seed to the region. This action saved on individual freight costs for the three farms involved. The shipment was completed last week.

Beryl Brookford:
It wasn’t… a large volume from any one place. But together, they filled the trailer. The key was… timing the return leg correctly.

2. Delivery Route Adjustments (Item led by Nico Poppycombe)
Nico provided an update on Meadow Mutual Aid’s response to the Brickway Bridge closure for repairs. The entire weekly delivery map for the Brickway Junction area was revised to account for the detour. Two extra volunteers were recruited to cover the longer routes. The new map has been in use for the past three delivery cycles.

Nico Poppycombe:
We had to look at every stop—the bakery, the senior residences, the clinic drop point. The detour adds about twenty minutes per loop, so we needed more hands. It’s running smoothly now.

3. Soil Health & Orchard Support (Item led by Ivy Hedgeton)
Ivy described work with a Blue Orchard apple grove that was showing signs of distress. Leaf tissue tests were conducted, which identified a specific potassium deficiency. Ivy recommended a tailored application of wood ash to correct the soil imbalance. The recommendation was delivered to the orchard manager two weeks ago.

Ivy Hedgeton:
The leaves were small and showed marginal scorching. The test confirmed it wasn’t a water or nitrogen issue. Wood ash is a good source of potassium here, and it’s locally available. We’ll monitor the new growth in the spring.

4. Mill Equipment Maintenance (Item led by Pia Reedford)
Pia reported on a maintenance issue at the Tinsmith Row Grain Mill. A sifter screen in the secondary flour line was torn, affecting the fineness grade of the output. Pia located a spare mesh roll in storage and performed the replacement. The repair restored the specified grade for that production batch.

Pia Reedford:
The mesh had worn thin along one seam. It’s a standard size, so I had the spare. Took about an hour to swap out and retension. The batch after that met spec.

5. Community Communication & Translation (Item led by Iolo Ashcombe)
Iolo shared work completed for the Gate Quarter library. The library’s frequently asked questions document regarding its upcoming roof renovation and temporary closure was translated. Iolo ensured the schedule for the closure and the locations of alternative services were clearly communicated in three languages: the primary language of the Emerald City, and the Munchkin and Winkie dialects commonly spoken in the district.

Iolo Ashcombe:
The important details were the dates of closure and where to return books. I worked to make the instructions for the temporary pickup station very precise, so there would be no confusion.

6. Open Discussion & Next Steps
A brief discussion followed on the potential for future freight consolidation between Gillikin growers and other network circles. Nico suggested a preliminary comparison of seasonal delivery volumes could be useful. Rowan Bell agreed to facilitate an initial conversation with the Brickway Transit Union in the next quarter.

Action Items:
- Beryl Brookford will circulate the cost-saving figures from the potato consolidation to the cooperative’s board by February 10.
- Nico Poppycombe will share the revised delivery map with the Network’s logistics working group for reference.
- Rowan Bell will contact the Brickway Transit Union to schedule a exploratory meeting on freight coordination for March 2025.

Meeting adjourned at 4:15 PM.
