The baker wanted a gap, not another handle

SYNTHETIC SOURCE — original fictional text; no real event or publication.
Format: conversation_transcript
Theme: Innovation in Oz

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Pavel, you brought a drawing to the repair co-op. Martin says it was mostly arrows.

Pavel Dunn:
Useful arrows. They showed where my fingers weren't fitting. We stack clean, cool, empty trays on a shelf before the morning work starts. The bottom edges sit so close together that I used to lift the whole stack to get the first one free.

Martin Woodward:
I offered him a handle. He looked at me as if I'd offered to put a bell on it.

Pavel Dunn:
You offered a very nice handle for the wrong problem. I didn't need help carrying a tray. I needed a gap under the stack.

Martin Woodward:
So we made two removable wooden spacers for the storage shelf. They're outside the food-preparation area. Nothing is attached to a baking tray, and nothing goes near an oven.

Pavel Dunn:
I brought one of our ordinary trays over and showed Martin how I lift it. The first spacers were too close together. He moved them, and I could get my fingers underneath without lifting the rest.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Have they been back to the bakery?

Pavel Dunn:
They've been on the clean-tray storage shelf all week. I take them out when I wipe the shelf. That's another reason I didn't want them fixed down.

Martin Woodward:
He rejected my first sketch and improved the second one. I'm allowing him to call that design work.

Pavel Dunn:
How generous. You did round the corners nicely.

Martin Woodward:
That was for me. I kept catching my sleeve on the first pair in the workshop.

Rowan Bell — community manager:
Is this becoming a product?

Pavel Dunn:
It is becoming a thing I stop thinking about every morning. That's plenty.

Martin Woodward:
We kept the measurements. If someone has a similar shelf, we can look at theirs. I'm not mailing out a universal bakery solution.

Pavel Dunn:
Good. Then you won't need a green seal on the box.

Martin Woodward:
I'd settle for the loaf you promised.

Pavel Dunn:
On the bench. Away from the sawdust, before you ask.
