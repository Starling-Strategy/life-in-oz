CLAY HOLLOW – Noticed a bit of sagging on the north wall shelving at Cress Clay Works last week. Daniel said he’d caught it himself before anything shifted. Got it braced with a length of angle iron screwed right into the wall studs. Said it was holding a full stack of those fifty-pound clay bags, the buff stoneware blend. No spill, no fuss.

Separately, from the delivery rounds: a few of the other suppliers here have mentioned Daniel’s portable jump-starter. It’s become something of a neighborhood loaner when a delivery van or a flatbed won’t turn over on a damp morning. Saves a call for a tow every time. One of the glaze drivers said it got her back on the road to the Emerald City with a full load last month.
