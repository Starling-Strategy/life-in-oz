Two bits of work completed:

For the Timber Verge Repair Co-op: I finished the restoration on that industrial overlocker for the Winkie adult ed textile program. It was donated in pieces, missing several tension discs and with a badly bent handwheel. I sourced compatible parts from two retired machines, machined a new handwheel spindle, and recalibrated the stitch differential. It runs quietly now and can handle heavy denim. The instructor picked it up yesterday; she said the class can finally teach professional seam finishing techniques.

Separately, Jude Quadstead over in Westgate Mills reports he replaced the rusted-out steel grate at the Reed Flats dairy wash-down channel. The old one was a hazard—someone's boot went through last month. He measured the channel, cut and welded a new grate from heavier-gauge stock, and sealed the welds to resist the wash-down chemicals. The dairy manager says it's solid as the road now.
