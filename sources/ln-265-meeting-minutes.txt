Wizard's Office Liaisons Circle
Meeting Minutes
Date: 12 June 2025
Present: Magnus Greendale, Heath Gatecombe, Tomas Saffronley, Coralie Greenshaw, Mott Peppercombe, Juniper Hazelby, Rowan Bell (Community Manager)

1. Welcome and Opening Remarks
Rowan Bell opened the meeting at 7:05 p.m. The agenda was accepted without amendment.

2. Review of Member Actions and Updates
2.1 Magnus Greendale reported on his work to revise the instructions for requesting a replacement household recycling bin. The new instructions are clearer and now include a simple diagram. He noted that the average call time to the civic works department for this issue has decreased by two minutes since the change. A clerk from the department confirmed the reduction.

2.2 Heath Gatecombe provided an update on his proposal for the Brickway Junction library branch. He plans to advocate for the installation of an after-hours book return drop box. The request will be framed to accommodate residents who work during the library's open hours. He will prepare a formal proposal for the next Library Advisory Board meeting.

2.3 Tomas Saffronley reported on the completion of a small civic grant project in Cobble Ford. The grant, which he facilitated, allowed the hamlet to repair a historic stone well cover that had become a safety hazard. The work is finished, and the well is now safely covered.

2.4 Coralie Greenshaw shared that she has created a simple glossary explaining common healthcare terms like 'co-pay' and 'referral' in plain language. It is a single laminated sheet. Three Quadling clinics are now keeping copies at their front desks. She noted that several patients have commented that it helps them understand their paperwork better.

2.5 Mott Peppercombe gave a brief report from the Brickway Works bridge crew. He stated that his crew recently used a quick-set concrete mix to fill and level a cracked concrete approach slab on a little-used farm track bridge near Bluebell Crossing. The work was done to prevent further deterioration from water ingress. The repair is holding.

2.6 Regarding Juniper Hazelby, Magnus Greendale mentioned that he and others had heard about her contribution to last year's Gillikin Light Festival. He said the word among liaisons was that Juniper provided the precise metal framework for a large kinetic sculpture that was a centerpiece of the event. Juniper Hazelby confirmed she had worked on the project but noted the framework was a subcontract from the main fabricator. She did not elaborate further.

3. Discussion: Improving Communication Between Offices and Residents
3.1 Magnus Greendale initiated a discussion on the persistent issue of public notices being written in overly formal language. He stated, "We get fewer complaints when the words are simpler. My recycling bin project proved that. But getting other departments to adopt the practice is slow."

3.2 Coralie Greenshaw agreed, relating it to her glossary work. "People shouldn't need a translator for basic services," she said.

3.3 Heath Gatecombe suggested that liaisons could compile examples of successful plain-language revisions from their respective areas to create a shared resource. Tomas Saffronley noted that such a resource would need approval from the central communications office to be used widely.

3.4 Juniper Hazelby asked Tomas to repeat the point about central office approval. After he did, she said, "Right. So we'd need a spec for that resource before we build it."

3.5 Action: Magnus Greendale and Coralie Greenshaw will draft two or three examples of revised public notices/forms for review at the next meeting.

4. Liaison Support Requests
4.1 Mott Peppercombe raised an issue about the standard forms for reporting minor bridge wear. He finds the current form has too many fields irrelevant to initial field assessment. "My crew spots a crack, they need to log the location and size, not write an essay on drainage patterns from five years ago. That comes later."

4.2 Tomas Saffronley said he would inquire about the possibility of a simplified 'field observation' version of the form. Heath Gatecombe offered to provide feedback from the ward perspective on what information is most critical for initial reporting.

4.3 Action: Tomas Saffronley will follow up with the Infrastructure Forms office and report back.

5. Any Other Business
5.1 Rowan Bell reminded the group of the Network's Summer Social on July 20th.

5.2 Juniper Hazelby asked if there was a standard process for liaisons to share technical specifications for public works projects across regions (e.g., bridge components, signage brackets). A brief discussion confirmed there is not a formal channel. Tomas Saffronley suggested this could be added to the agenda for a future meeting focused on inter-regional projects.

6. Next Meeting
Agreed for Wednesday, 10 July 2025, at 7:00 p.m. at the Emerald City Chamber meeting room.

Meeting adjourned at 8:30 p.m.
Minutes recorded by Rowan Bell.
