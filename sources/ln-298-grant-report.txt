FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

BRICKWAY JUNCTION, MUNCHKIN COUNTRY – August 2025 – The Brickway Junction Makers Space, supported by the Emerald City Leadership Network’s Community Action Fund, has completed two key projects to enhance its facility and programming as the season turns.

In late July, co-founder Dinah Haywick organized and led a basic bicycle maintenance clinic. Over two evenings, twelve participants learned hands-on skills, including patching inner tubes and adjusting brakes, using a set of six workshop stands donated by a member of the Brickway Transit Union. The clinic filled to capacity, with several participants bringing in their own bikes for repair. 'We had folks from teenagers to retirees, all with the same puzzled look at a flat tire,' Haywick noted. 'By the end, they had the confidence to fix it themselves, and the space was buzzing.'

Separately, in preparation for the cooler months ahead, Haywick sourced, cleaned, and safety-tested three second-hand radiant space heaters for the Makers Space’s common area. The units were acquired through a local reuse exchange. Each heater was disassembled, cleaned of dust and debris, had its electrical cords inspected, and was run through a full operational cycle to ensure safe, reliable function. 'The goal was warmth without worry,' Haywick said. 'Now when people come in on a chilly evening to work on a project, they can focus on their hands, not on their chills.'

The grant funds covered the cost of clinic materials (patch kits, lubricants, instructional handouts) and the acquisition and minor parts for the heaters. The labor was provided by Haywick and volunteer members of the space.

An unfinished element of the grant scope is the installation of additional task lighting over the main workbenches; this remains planned for the coming month as the necessary fixtures are sourced.

About the Brickway Junction Makers Space: Founded in 2024, the space provides tools, workstations, and shared expertise for residents of Brickway Junction and surrounding communities to repair, build, and learn. It is a member circle of the Emerald City Leadership Network.
