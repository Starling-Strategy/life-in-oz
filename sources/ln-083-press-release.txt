September 2024, Emerald City – The Emerald City Leadership Network’s Young Founders circle, a group dedicated to supporting the next generation of business and community leaders across Oz, is highlighting two recent, tangible successes from its members. These accomplishments underscore the circle’s focus on applied problem-solving and cross-country collaboration.

At the Saffron Flats Can Works in Timber Verge, frequent minor jams on a main filling line conveyor were causing small batches of product to be scrapped each shift. Ivo Fernby, a floor lead at the Works, identified the issue as a misaligned timing sensor. ‘The jams were small, but they added up—waste and downtime,’ Fernby said. ‘It wasn’t a glamorous fix, just a careful recalibration. Now the line runs smoother, and we’re scrapping less.’

Meanwhile, in the Emerald City, Gus Poppyridge of Poppyridge Translations applied his precise language skills beyond interpretation. He was consulted on a contract between a Winkie Country metal fabricator and an Emerald City theatre group. The original delivery and liability clauses were creating confusion. Poppyridge provided clear, mutually agreeable revisions. ‘My work is about making sure intent is understood,’ Poppyridge noted. ‘In this case, that meant preventing a future misunderstanding that could have strained a good partnership.’
