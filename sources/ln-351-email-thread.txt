Lantern count discrepancy

Theme: Festival Committee

Lorna Plumshaw:
From: Lorna Plumshaw, Volunteer Lead, Festival Committee
To: Kerensa Lanternstead, Lanternstead Tinware
Subject: Lantern Frames for the Procession
Date: 15 October 2025

Dear Kerensa,

Thank you for your work on the lantern frames for the Festival of Light. The delivery arrived at the Gate Quarter store as arranged.

Our inventory today shows only one hundred and fifty frames present. This is a significant shortfall from the two hundred confirmed in the committee minutes of last month. I’ve had two volunteers double-check the back room and the main storage shelves.

We proceeded with the procession using the frames we had, and it was well-received, though we had to space the lanterns more widely along the Brickway than planned.

Could you please confirm the quantity you dispatched? If there was a partial shipment, we should account for the remainder.

Regards,
Lorna

Kerensa Lanternstead:
From: Kerensa Lanternstead, Lanternstead Tinware
To: Lorna Plumshaw, Volunteer Lead, Festival Committee
Subject: Re: Lantern Frames for the Procession
Date: 16 October 2025

Lorna,

I received your note. I am certain of the count. I made two hundred frames. My apprentice and I packed them all into the same four crates, which were collected by the carrier from Tinsmith Row. I watched them loaded.

If your store holds only one hundred and fifty, then fifty must be somewhere on the premises. Perhaps they were moved to a different area upon arrival, or set aside for inspection? They are all stamped with our workshop mark—a small lantern—on the inside rim.

The procession looked beautiful from the reports here, even with the wider spacing. But the frames exist. I would be happy to speak with the storekeeper there to help locate them.

Yours,
Kerensa

Lorna Plumshaw:
From: Lorna Plumshaw, Volunteer Lead, Festival Committee
To: Kerensa Lanternstead, Lanternstead Tinware
Subject: Re: Lantern Frames for the Procession
Date: 17 October 2025

Kerensa,

Thank you for your reply. I have now walked the entire store with the head clerk. We found no additional crates, nor any frames loose on shelves. We saw no stamp inside the rims of the frames we have; perhaps it was not applied to all?

The carrier’s docket, which I have here, lists four crates. It does not list a piece count. The storekeeper recalls receiving and signing for four crates. He does not recall their relative fullness.

Without the frames present, I cannot amend our inventory. I have recorded your account in our notes. We will plan for a different count next year.

Lorna

Kerensa Lanternstead:
From: Kerensa Lanternstead, Lanternstead Tinware
To: Lorna Plumshaw, Volunteer Lead, Festival Committee
Subject: Re: Lantern Frames for the Procession
Date: 19 October 2025

Lorna,

The stamp is on every one. It is light, but it is there. If you have the frames, you have the stamp.

I made two hundred. They left here. I do not know where they are now. I am sorry the count does not match, but my count is correct.

Perhaps we should leave it there.

Kerensa
