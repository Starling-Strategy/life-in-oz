# Generation materials

Adaptable authoring inputs, not a generation service. These files contain fictional premises and desired coverage; do not give them to a system being tested on extracting facts from source documents.

- [Modern-world roster](../world/people.json): stable names, roles, homes, backgrounds and acquaintance links. Backgrounds are invented premises, not independently supported evidence.
- [Initial recipe](recipe.json): 12 documents for eight selected members.
- [Expansion recipe](expansion-recipe.json): 35 documents for the remaining members.
- [Expansion experience seeds](expansion-experience-seeds.json): authoring premises, not reference answers.
- [Oscar supplement](oscar-supplement-recipe.json): one additional document describing a distinct episode.
- [Author prompt](author-prompt.md): prose and format instructions.
- [Style examples](style-examples.md): illustrative writing samples outside the source corpus.
- [Coverage audit prompt](coverage-audit-prompt.md): extraction instructions for proposing reference experiences.

These are cleaned adaptations of the retained authoring materials. They cover community-life, not a complete reconstruction of the five book-adventures supplements. They still refer to a superseded pilot where needed to exclude repeated creative premises; that pilot is not part of the release.

For a new collection, change the cast, themes and document briefs. Generate bounded drafts, review their continuity and attribution, then create annotations from the preserved documents. Keep raw runs private to your working area and check what you publish. No real contact details, invented live links or real-recording claims belong in a fictional source.

See [the method and its limitations](../docs/method.md). No provider connection or paid generation is needed to use the released data.
