## The book that inspired the setting

L. Frank Baum's *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz* (1900), available as [Project Gutenberg ebook #55](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/55), inspired the names, setting and motifs. Project Gutenberg identifies that edition as **public domain in the USA**. Check the law where you use it; this notice is not a worldwide copyright determination.

The original ebook is linked, not bundled or relicensed. Project Gutenberg's name and distribution terms are separate from this repository's CC0 dedication. We imply no affiliation with or endorsement by Project Gutenberg, Baum's estate, or any film or stage adaptation.

The modern scenes, occupations, settlements and dialogue are newly invented. Some names and motifs go beyond a literal reading of the first novel. In particular, "Oscar Diggs" is not presented here as a name established in ebook #55.
