# The modern-world roster

[people.json](people.json) contains 50 invented adult members of the Munchkin Association Alumni. It supplies authoring premises: names, roles, homes, backgrounds, speaking styles, inspirations and acquaintance links.

It is a starting point for making related fictional material, not evidence that every listed background appears in a document. Rowan Bell is a fictional moderator outside the 50-member roster and appears in the actual community-life sources as well as writing examples.

This roster belongs to **community-life**. The book-adventures collection reuses some cast identities under different physical and narrative assumptions. Do not merge those histories automatically.

Do not supply this file to a blind document-extraction test. Its role and relationship fields give away information that the tested system should recover, or decline to infer, from the documents themselves.
